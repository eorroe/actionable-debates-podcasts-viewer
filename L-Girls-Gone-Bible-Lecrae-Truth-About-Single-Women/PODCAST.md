# Podcast Title

Lecrae - Girls Gone Bible Give Lecrae the Truth About Single Women

## Overview

In this episode of The Deep End with Lecrae, Ari and An from Girls Gone Bible share testimonies about finding freedom in Christ through purity, identity, and community. They discuss how young Christian women can navigate Los Angeles and the entertainment industry, social media, singleness, and dating while staying anchored in Jesus. The conversation covers laying down sin, singleness as a gift, building godly community, and finding identity in Christ.

## When to Follow Podcast

- When you are a young single Christian woman seeking biblical guidance on identity, dating, and purity
- When you feel pressure from social media, Los Angeles and the entertainment industry, or cultural Christianity in the Western church to perform or appear perfect
- When you are struggling with mental illness, anxiety, panic disorder, or alcoholism and want to hear how Jesus brings freedom
- When you want to hear honest testimonies about singleness, waiting seasons, and not finding worth in a relationship
- When you are called to acting, music, or entertainment and need biblical wisdom on staying on mission for Jesus

## Discussed

### Topic 1: Finding Your Identity in Christ Alone

#### Steps

##### Step 1: Stop putting your identity into another person

Do not look to a boyfriend, husband, or anyone else to define who you are. Your identity must be rooted in knowing Jesus and knowing yourself through Him. Ari says that putting your identity into another person always leaves you empty.

##### Step 2: Spend time alone with Jesus first before pursuing relationships

Ari emphasizes that she spent two years with Jesus alone, which is how she truly came to know Him and know herself. Only then can someone else be added to your life.

##### Step 3: Check your heart posture constantly

Lecrae's motto is to do a constant, daily check of the heart: ask God to search your heart and give you a clean heart. Make sure you are not making your faith, your ministry, or your relationships about yourself rather than about Jesus.

##### Step 4: Be as low to the ground as possible

Your motto should be humility. Ari's motto is, "Be as low to the ground as possible." The lower you get, the more he decreases in your life. Do not chase applause, recognition, or glory—only Jesus is meant to carry glory.

##### Step 5: Keep your strength as your weakness

Lecrae encourages the hosts: "Keep your strength as your weakness." Do not let the Christian industry or social pressure make you pretend to be perfect. Your strength becomes a weakness when it is used as a facade.

#### Examples

- Ari's story of being in a relationship where she had not yet laid down sexual sin. She felt spiritually dead, with no peace, no joy, and no direction—until she surrendered completely.
- Ari describes putting her identity into a relationship as the reason she came up empty. She found freedom only after choosing to walk with Jesus alone first, spending two years with Him.
- Lecrae shares his own experience of living for applause and describes the enemy's agenda as making believers "aesthetically pleasing, be entertaining, and say the right things" rather than genuinely surrendered.

#### Best Practices

- Anchor your identity in knowing Jesus, not in a relationship, career, or social media following
- Spend intentional time alone with God before pursuing romantic relationships
- Check your heart posture constantly to ensure everything you do is for God's glory, not your own
- Keep a humble posture—be "as low to the ground as possible"
- Let go of the need for applause, recognition, or being seen as successful in the world's eyes
- Keep your strength as your weakness; do not perform perfection for the Christian industry

#### Keep In Mind

- Putting your identity into another person always leaves you empty. True fulfillment comes from Jesus alone.
- The entertainment industry in Los Angeles and the lights, cameras, and action will never fulfill you. Hollywood will "chew you up, spit you out, use you, promise you things that it never has any intention of bringing you." You are not meant to carry glory—only Jesus is.
- Lecrae's encouragement: "Keep your strength as your weakness." Don't let the Christian industry or social pressure make you pretend to be perfect.

#### Security & Safety Notes

- Do not drink alcohol or use relationships as a way to cope with pain or trauma when the only thing that makes you feel better. These will only deepen your bondage. Ari drank "because I wanted to be okay," and it led to full-blown alcoholism.
- If you are in a relationship where you feel spiritually dead, confused, or in bondage, it is a sign that sin is present. Ari wrote in her journal, "Jesus, I'm dead. I am spiritually dead. I am just getting by." Sexual sin specifically clouds your ability to see Jesus clearly.
- When you feel pressure to perform, look perfect, or hide your struggles, remember that transparency and vulnerability set people free.

#### Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Putting your identity and worth into a romantic partner instead of Jesus.
  **Solution:** Spend time alone with Jesus first. Know Him, know yourself, and let relationships be added rather than needed.

- **Problem:** Chasing fame, the spotlight, or the approval of others in entertainment or online spaces.
  **Solution:** Remember you are created to be on mission for Jesus, not to be a star. Hollywood will "chew you up, spit you out, and promise things it never intends to bring you." Stay surrounded by godly people who will speak truth to you.

- **Problem:** Comparing yourself to others on social media or in church and feeling inadequate, shameful, or "too far gone."
  **Solution:** Understand that your inadequacy comes from shame, not from reality. Get into godly community where people will pray for you and speak life over you. Community is far more gracious than social media.

### Topic 2: Sexual Purity as the Path to Freedom and Clarity

#### Steps

##### Step 1: Recognize that sexual sin perverts your mind

Sexual sin is unique because it is a sin against your own body. It opens doors to the demonic and prevents you from seeing Jesus clearly. You cannot know how to be in the will of God while living in sexual sin. You will be confused and directionless.

##### Step 2: Lay down your life of sin completely

One foot in and one foot out will not work. You cannot be walking with the Lord but be living in habitual sin. God does not give rules to restrict you—He commands obedience out of love. When you truly decide to follow Him, that is when you are set free.

##### Step 3: Get godly community around you

Accountability from mature believers is essential. You cannot overcome sexual sin on your own. Lecrae and the Girls Gone Bible hosts both emphasize the need for people who will tell you the hard things you do not want to hear. Ari says, "I need your help. I'm done."

##### Step 4: Walk in the freedom Jesus purchased

When you lay down sin, your mind is freed. Obsessive thoughts, depression, and tormenting thoughts can leave. This freedom is not your own doing—it is Jesus lifting you up when you get on your hands and knees. Ari says that when she laid down the sin, "my mind is free. I don't have tormenting thoughts. Obsessive compulsive thinking."

#### Examples

- Ari's testimony of being in a relationship and writing in her journal, "Jesus, I'm dead. I am spiritually dead. I am just getting by. I have nothing in me. I don't know what's going on. I don't feel you're nowhere to be found. I know you're here, but I have separated myself from you." She had no peace, no joy, and no direction until she ended the relationship and surrendered to purity.
- The story of being confronted with two godly, pure young women in Texas. Ari says, "I'm like confronted with like wow this is everything that I'm not." For the next couple of weeks, she was in a relationship and extremely convicted but did not know what to do or how to stop. She did not know who she was without being promiscuous or dressing a certain way. She had a vision of her innocent younger self in a house in Connecticut, and felt God say she could have that innocence back. She called her boyfriend and said, "I'm done. I can't do this anymore."
- Lecrae shares a personal story of being intimate with a girl the night before a YMCA show. He woke up feeling like he wanted to die. He says, "I didn't even realize I had fragile mental health. So, I'm piling on shame and guilt and the conviction of the spirit onto a fragile mind. That's right. And it was torturous." He explains that Satan uses sexual sin to keep Christians in shame and bondage, silencing their witness: "Satan keeps you in bondage is it's how it can keep your mouth closed from being a voice for the Lord because you're living in shame and bondage."

#### Best Practices

- Understand that sexual sin blinds you to Jesus. Purity of heart is required to see God.
- Lay down any relationship or habit that is keeping you from fully following Jesus. No half-measures. One foot in and one foot out will not work.
- Surround yourself with godly community who will hold you accountable and speak truth.
- Trust that God's commands are for your good, not your harm. He loves you and wants you free.
- Be honest about your struggles. Transparency sets others free and breaks the enemy's scheme of shame.

#### Keep In Mind

- Sexual sin is not just "like other sins"—it has a unique power to pervert your mind. You will be confused and directionless if you are living in sexual sin.
- The freedom Ari experienced—no more obsessive thoughts, no more tormenting mind—came only after she fully surrendered. those six months led to one moment where like the stars aligned. Me and God come into alignment. We come into agreement that it's over.
- Lecrae warns that Satan keeps Christians in bondage through sexual sin so they cannot be a voice for the Lord. Their shame keeps their mouth closed.

#### Security & Safety Notes

- If you are in a sexually impure relationship, you are at spiritual risk. Your mind cannot see Jesus clearly.
- Half-hearted repentance does not bring freedom. You must fully lay down the sin, get accountability, and trust God for transformation.
- Do not toy with sexual sin. It is a gateway to deeper bondage, shame, and confusion.

#### Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Trying to walk with the Lord while remaining in a sexually sinful relationship or lifestyle.
  **Solution:** You must end the relationship and fully surrender. "You cannot be walking with the Lord but be living in habitual sin."

- **Problem:** Thinking you can overcome sexual sin on your own without community or accountability.
  **Solution:** Get godly people around you who will speak truth. "I need your help. I'm done."

- **Problem:** Letting shame from past sexual sin keep you silent and unable to share your testimony.
  **Solution:** that's what testimonies do. They bear the image of Jesus Christ. Share your story everywhere—Uber, work, Starbucks.

### Topic 3: Singleness as a Gift, Not a Curse

#### Steps

##### Step 1: Choose to see singleness as a gift and a blessing

Do not look at singleness as a curse or a state of lack. Paul said he wishes everyone would stay single like him. Singleness is an incredible opportunity to serve the Lord and do things couples cannot do.

##### Step 2: Do not put your identity or validation into finding a mate

Ari warns that putting your identity into another person will always leave you empty. She says, "I don't need someone. Most women need men. A lot of men need women and that's why they collapse." Spend time with Jesus, know yourself, and let a relationship be an addition, not a necessity.

##### Step 3: Use your singleness to serve the Lord wholeheartedly

Lecrae shares that through his singleness, he has been able to go to incredible places and connect deeply with God. He says, "The places I've gotten to go to, the friends I've made, it's just me and him." You have freedom and flexibility—use it for the kingdom.

##### Step 4: Date with the right priorities and through godly community

When you are ready to date, do not swipe or chase appearances. Tell the godliest people in your life you are looking, and let them help you find someone. Look for someone captivated by Jesus, not someone who merely checks boxes.

##### Step 5: Prioritize character over appearance, job, or social status

Do not prioritize looks, job title, or social media followers. Ask: Who will hold you up when your faith falls? Who will make the hard decisions when tragedy strikes? Who will pray with you when you lose your way? Ari says, "I want the guy who's going to help my dad if he falls, who's going to help me, who's going to put their hands on me and pray for me if God forbid something happens where I lose my faith."

#### Examples

- Ari's story of an almost four-year breakup. She chose to heal with Jesus instead of jumping into a new relationship with a band-aid over the wound. She says, "I can wake up in peace. I love myself, and I'm comfortable with myself now. And it happened later in life for me."
- Lecrae's philosophy: "Marriage is hard. Single is hard, choose your heart. Marriage is a blessing. Singleness is a blessing. Choose your blessing." Both states have difficulty, but both are blessings when viewed through the lens of kingdom living.
- Ari describes how she used to want a man who was spiritually gifted and could pray for her—but after pursuing purity, she realized she literally did not care what they looked like or did for a job. She only cared about who they were in Christ. "Like that's truly and like you can testify that's like literally all I cared about."

#### Best Practices

- See singleness as an opportunity, not a deficiency. It is a gift to be unwrapped.
- Do not let the pressure to get married young make you compromise on godly character.
- Date through word of mouth and godly community, not through apps or superficial means.
- Look for someone captivated by Jesus, not someone who merely shares your interests.
- Speak life over the men in your life. Encourage them to lead. Give them space to step up.

#### Keep In Mind

- Everyone is a little annoying. What matters is that your future spouse is governed by the Spirit of God, yields to Jesus, and has humility to say "I'm sorry."
- If you are single, you have freedom to go anywhere, serve anywhere, and say yes to opportunities that married people cannot.
- Do not compare your singleness journey to others who got married young. God's timing is different for everyone.

#### Security & Safety Notes

- Do not date someone who is not walking with Jesus. Missionary dating rarely works, and the standard should be someone who wants to look like Jesus.
- Do not rush into marriage out of fear of being single. Choose your heart. Choose your blessing.
- Do not let the world's standards (swipe culture, looks, status) dictate who you date. These are empty and deceptive.

#### Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Feeling pressure to be in a relationship because everyone else is married.
  **Solution:** Remember that singleness is a gift. Use it to grow with Jesus and serve the kingdom. Marriage will come in God's timing.

- **Problem:** Choosing a partner based on appearance, job, or social status rather than their relationship with Jesus.
  **Solution:** Prioritize character and faith above all else. Ask: Who will stand by you in a hospital room? Who will help your family in crisis?

- **Problem:** Wanting a leader but not empowering men to lead.
  **Solution:** As women, we must speak to men as if they are already the leaders we want them to be. Give them space, confidence, and words of life.

### Topic 4: Transparency, Testimony, and Navigating Scrutiny

#### Steps

##### Step 1: Be authentic and vulnerable about your struggles

Do not put on a facade of perfection. Share your real story—your pain, your mental health battles, your wilderness seasons. Your testimony sets people free.

##### Step 2: Expect and process criticism with wisdom

You will receive harsh comments online, even from people who claim to be Christians. Recognize that many critics are projecting their own sin and brokenness. Do not take their opinions to heart—especially if their profiles reveal they do not even live by the standards they are judging you for.

##### Step 3: Prioritize in-person community over social media comments

Build real relationships with godly people who know you, love you, and will speak truth to you. Community is patient, consistent, and loving. Social media is not.

##### Step 4: Use your platform to preach the gospel everywhere

Your testimony is not just for your podcast or social media. Share it in the back of an Uber, at Starbucks, at your job, everywhere. People are suffering in silence and need to hear your story.

##### Step 5: Stay humble and keep checking your heart

The moment you start making your ministry or platform about yourself, you have missed it. Be as low to the ground as possible. Keep Jesus at the forefront.

#### Examples

- Ari almost did not release the first episode of Girls Gone Bible because she was terrified of being exposed. She shared that she almost took her own life, came from generations of mental illness, and had a facade of perfection on Instagram. A girl later messaged them saying their testimony helped her stop taking her own life.
- The Girls Gone Bible hosts describe getting criticized for modesty early on, but realizing that even through harsh comments, the Lord could work through broken people to bring accountability.
- Lecrae tells a story of a woman who ripped him apart on modesty. He looked at her profile afterward—her profile picture was wild, like no clothes. The hosts realized she was projecting: "I'm not serious about Jesus, and I know I should be, but you say that you are. So, be better."

#### Best Practices

- Share your testimony everywhere—not just on a stage or podcast. Real people need real hope.
- Do not be ashamed of your past. Your redemption story is powerful and points to Jesus.
- Let your spiritual formation happen in community first, not in the public square. Authenticity is born when you are not performing for an audience.
- Recognize that people who criticize you harshly online are often projecting their own guilt and unmet standards.
- Keep your platform and ministry about Jesus, not about yourself. "Is this going to glorify Him?" should be the question.

#### Keep In Mind

- People are broken. depression's at an all-time high. More suicides than ever. Your vulnerability can be the thing that saves someone's life.
- Social media creates a fake aesthetic of perfect Christianity. Do not buy into it. The real power is in the raw, unfiltered truth.
- The enemy wants you to be aesthetically pleasing, entertaining, and saying the right things. But Jesus calls you to deny yourself, die to self, and share the truth.

#### Security & Safety Notes

- Do not let social media comments destroy your heart. If you need to look at comments, remember that many are not even from Christians, and most are projection.
- Do not perform Christianity for an audience. Your faith should be genuine and rooted in private relationship with Jesus, not in public perception.
- Keep your circle small with people who know the real you. Let their validation matter more than the comments of strangers.

#### Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Being paralyzed by fear of what others will think, so you stay silent instead of sharing your testimony.
  **Solution:** Remember that someone is suffering in silence right now who needs to hear your story. Transparency sets people free.

- **Problem:** Letting social media comments define your worth or make you doubt your calling.
  **Solution:** Community is more gracious than social media. Look to the 10 people closest to you for validation, not to strangers online.

- **Problem:** Making your platform, podcast, or ministry about yourself rather than about Jesus.
  **Solution:** Constantly check your heart posture. Ask: "Is this glorifying Him?" Be low to the ground. Stay humble.

- **Problem:** Comparing yourself to others who appear to have it all together.
  **Solution:** The perfect-looking Christians on social media are often the most broken. Your mess is part of your ministry.
