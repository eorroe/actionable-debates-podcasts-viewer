# Debate - Faith, Purpose & Prosperity

## Overview

This debate explores how business acumen meets biblical wisdom, featuring Phillip Anthony Mitchell, Tim Timberlake, and Toure Roberts at Investfest. The panel discusses prosperity theology, stewardship, character development, discernment of God's opportunities versus good opportunities, and maintaining intimacy with God amid success. The central thesis is that wealth and influence are tools for purpose, not ends in themselves, and that character and intimacy with God determine whether you can sustain the blessings you receive.

## When to Follow Debate

- When building a business or ministry with biblical principles
- When discerning between business or ministry opportunities
- When struggling with identity beyond your gifts or platform
- When wanting to advance spiritually and financially without compromising
- When learning to steward success and influence

## Glossary / Prerequisite Terms To Know Before Following Debate

- **Stewardship:** The responsible management and multiplication of resources God has entrusted to you
- **God Opportunity:** An opportunity that aligns with your God-given purpose and brings peace
- **Good Opportunity:** An attractive opportunity that may not align with your divine purpose
- **Prophetic Word:** A specific revelation or instruction from God about your purpose and calling
- **Intimacy with God:** A deep, honest, private relationship with God that transforms character
- **Convictions:** Established biblical principles that govern your yeses and nos
- **Idolatry of Gifts:** When you value your gift, platform, or business more than the God who gave it

## Steps For Each Topic and Each Topic's View

### Topic 1 / View 1: God is Not Against Financial Blessing

#### Step 1: Audit Current Stewardship

Take inventory of your current resources, time, talents, and finances. Assess how well you are stewarding what God has already placed in your hands.

#### Step 2: Seek Wisdom Over Wealth

Before praying for increased financial blessing, pray for wisdom to manage your current resources. Ask God to show you where you can be more faithful with what you have.

#### Step 3: Eliminate Comparison and Jealousy

Identify any jealousy or covetousness toward others' financial success. Redirect that energy toward focusing on your own calling and path.

#### Step 4: Remain Faithful at Your Current Level

Demonstrate faithfulness with current blessings. God will trust you with greater resources when you prove trustworthy with smaller ones.

### Topic 1 / View 2: Wealth Is Individually Assigned According to Purpose

#### Step 1: Identify Your God-Given Purpose

Spend time in prayer and reflection to understand what God has specifically called you to do. Write this down.

#### Step 2: Evaluate Your Calling's Financial Demands

Understand whether your specific calling requires financial resources and to what level. Don't compare your assignment to others.

#### Step 3: Trust God's Assignment

Release any desire for wealth that is not assigned to your purpose. Trust that God will provide exactly what you need for what He has called you to do.

#### Step 4: Avoid Coveting Others' Assignments

When you see others blessed financially, remind yourself that their assignment may be different from yours. Stay focused on your own path.

### Topic 2 / View 1: Character Development Cannot Be Skipped

#### Step 1: Embrace the School of Life

Stop resisting pain, hardships, trials, and tribulations. Recognize these as God's tools for shaping your character.

#### Step 2: Prioritize Character Over Gifts

Regularly evaluate whether you are developing character as much as you are developing your gifts and talents. Character keeps what gifts acquire.

#### Step 3: Allow God to Shape You

Yield to God's process rather than rushing past difficulties. Trust that He is building the fortitude you need for future blessings.

#### Step 4: Protect Relationships and Influence

Remember that character determines whether you keep relationships and influence. Make character development a non-negotiable priority.

### Topic 2 / View 2: Advancement Requires Sacrificing Former Levels

#### Step 1: Identify Current Blessings to Sacrifice

List your current platforms, relationships, or blessings that may be holding you back from the next level. Ask God which ones need to be laid down.

#### Step 2: Lay It on the Altar

Place these blessings on the altar before God. Tell Him you trust Him with the outcome and you will not pick them back up until He tells you to.

#### Step 3: Die to the Current Level

Let go of attachment to your current status or blessings. True sacrifice means letting it stay down until God restores it.

#### Step 4: Prove Your Heart

Use this sacrifice as proof to God and yourself that He has your heart. This positions you for advancement to the next level.

### Topic 2 / View 3: God Delays Blessings to Develop Character

#### Step 1: Trust the Delay

When blessings seem delayed, remind yourself that God delays to protect and develop you. Trust that delayed blessings are right on time.

#### Step 2: Learn Contentment in Waiting

Practice being content in your current season. Don't rush ahead of God's timing.

#### Step 3: Enjoy the View

Find joy and fulfillment in the waiting process rather than just focusing on the destination. Let God teach you during the delay.

#### Step 4: Avoid Impulsive Decisions

When tempted to make rash decisions because of delay, remember that God's timing is perfect and your timing could be harmful.

### Topic 3 / View 1: Peace and Joy Discern God Opportunities

#### Step 1: Test Against Peace

When an opportunity arises, check your peace. If it robs you of peace, it is too expensive regardless of how attractive it seems.

#### Step 2: Align with Purpose

Evaluate whether the opportunity aligns with your God-given purpose, not just your passions or desires.

#### Step 3: Reject Peace-Robbing Opportunities

Practice saying no to opportunities that seem good but don't bring peace. No is a powerful word.

#### Step 4: Seek Joy Over Mere Happiness

Understand that God opportunities bring lasting joy and purpose alignment, while good opportunities only bring temporary happiness.

### Topic 3 / View 2: Avoid Being Thirsty - Intimacy Brings Discernment

#### Step 1: Evaluate Your Thirst Level

Evaluate your posture by asking: Do I chase every opportunity that opens? Do I feel anxious when doors close? Do I compare my platform to others? If yes, you may be operating from desperation rather than discernment.

#### Step 2: Cultivate Intimacy with God

Develop a deep, private relationship with God through prayer, honesty, and surrender. Intimacy is the source of true discernment.

#### Step 3: Learn to Say No

Practice saying no to open doors that don't align with your calling. No keeps you out of trouble.

#### Step 4: Let God Open the Right Doors

Stop chasing every open door. Trust God to open the doors you are supposed to walk through at the right time.

### Topic 3 / View 3: Govern by Written Prophetic Purpose

#### Step 1: Write Down Your Purpose

Spend time with God and write down what He has shown you about your purpose, calling, and vision.

#### Step 2: Filter All Opportunities Through Your Written Purpose

When any opportunity arises, compare it against your written purpose. If it doesn't align, it's a no.

#### Step 3: Treat Silence as No

If God has not clearly shown you an opportunity, treat it as a no. Silence is not approval.

#### Step 4: Trust Your Specific Assignment

Remember that you are not random. God knew you before birth and has a specific purpose for you. Trust that He will align opportunities with that purpose.

### Topic 3 / View 4: Core Convictions Protect from Compromise

#### Step 1: Establish Core Convictions

Before opportunities arise, establish biblical convictions that will govern your decisions. Write them down.

#### Step 2: Let Convictions Determine Yeses and Nos

When faced with a decision, run it through your convictions first. Let them determine your answer.

#### Step 3: Consider Eternal Accountability

Remember that you will give an account to God for every decision. Let this eternal perspective shape your choices.

#### Step 4: Be Willing to Decline Lucrative Opportunities

Be prepared to turn down lucrative opportunities if they compromise your convictions, witness, or calling.

### Topic 4 / View 1: Use Gifts for God's Glory, Not Self

#### Step 1: Evaluate Your Motives

Regularly examine whether you are using your gifts for self-promotion or for God's glory. Ask yourself who gets the attention.

#### Step 2: Think About the Recipient

Shift your focus from your idea or platform to the person who will be blessed by it. Think about the problem you are solving.

#### Step 3: Solve Problems, Not Just Make Money

Understand that money is the reward for a problem solved. Focus on the problem, not just the financial reward.

#### Step 4: Give Glory to the Giver

Remember that your gift came from God. Use it in a way that honors Him and points others to Him.

### Topic 4 / View 2: The Idol Test - Giver, Gift, Recipient

#### Step 1: Apply the Idol Test

When evaluating your relationship to your gift, ask: Do I think about the Giver more than the gift? Do I think about the recipient more than the gift itself?

#### Step 2: Reorder Your Affections

If you think about the gift more than the Giver or the recipient, your affections are misplaced. Reorder them.

#### Step 3: Focus on the Problem, Not the Idea

If you are more passionate about your business idea than the problem it solves, you have missed the point. Refocus on the problem.

#### Step 4: Align with God's Heart

Shift your focus from what's in your hands to what's in God's heart. Let His desires for people drive your use of gifts.

### Topic 4 / View 3: Lay Everything on the Altar

#### Step 1: Identify What Needs to Be Laid Down

List your business, platform, influence, ideas, and desires. Ask God which ones need to be placed on the altar.

#### Step 2: Physically or Verbally Lay It on the Altar

Actively place these things on the altar before God. Tell Him, "This all belongs to you. I'm fully committed to you."

#### Step 3: Trust God with Outcomes

Release control of the outcomes. Tell God you trust Him with whatever the future holds.

#### Step 4: Don't Pick It Back Up Until God Says So

Resist the urge to take back control. Wait for God to tell you when to pick anything back up.

### Topic 5 / View 1: Deep Private Relationship Protects from Bad Decisions

#### Step 1: Prioritize Private Time with God

Make time for God your first priority each day. This is not optional for those who want to steward influence well.

#### Step 2: Be Honest and Unsanitized

Talk to God honestly about your feelings, struggles, and desires. Don't hide behind empty religious clichés or sanitized theology when talking to God. Be raw and honest about your true feelings and struggles.

#### Step 3: Confess and Renounce Sin Regularly

Confess and renounce sin from your heart through honest prayer and repentance at least daily to maintain clean fellowship with God. Let Him search your heart.

#### Step 4: Let Intimacy Govern Decisions

Allow your intimate relationship with God to be the primary filter for all major decisions. Intimacy protects from being led astray.

### Topic 5 / View 2: Surrender Brokenness to God's Wholeness

#### Step 1: Open Up Fully to God

Don't hide your brokenness from God. Open up the deepest parts of yourself to Him, even the parts you don't understand.

#### Step 2: Yield Brokenness to God

Surrender your brokenness, pain, and imperfections to God. Trust that His wholeness can heal and restore you.

#### Step 3: Allow Transformation Through Intimacy

Let time with God change you. Like a married couple become one, intimacy with God should transform you into His image.

#### Step 4: Worship Through Surrender

Understand that true worship is surrender. Open yourself to God and let Him change you. This is the deepest form of intimacy.

### Topic 6 / View 1: Identity Is Not Your Gift or Work

#### Step 1: Ask Who You Are When Alone

Spend time alone asking, "Who am I when no one is watching? Who am I to me?" Don't define yourself by your work.

#### Step 2: Separate Gift from Identity

Recognize that your gift, platform, and work are not who you are. You are a son/daughter of God first.

#### Step 3: Let God Define You

Allow God to recognize you as His child rather than seeking identity from what you do or what others think of you.

#### Step 4: Value Yourself Beyond Your Work

Understand that there is more value to you than what your hands are doing. Your identity is in Christ, not your productivity.

### Topic 6 / View 2: Contentment in Abundance and Lack

#### Step 1: Learn Contentment in Every Season

Practice being content whether you are in abundance or lack. Make this a discipline in your life.

#### Step 2: Trust God's Timing in Delays

When waiting for breakthroughs, trust that God knows the end from the beginning. Delays are often for your development.

#### Step 3: Enjoy the Waiting Season

Don't just endure waiting - learn to enjoy the view. Let God teach you during the delay.

#### Step 4: Avoid Impulsive Decisions

When tempted to act impulsively because of delay or lack, remember that God's timing is perfect and rushing can cost you.
