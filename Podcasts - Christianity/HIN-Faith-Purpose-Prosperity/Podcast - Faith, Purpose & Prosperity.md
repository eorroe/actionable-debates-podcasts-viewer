# Faith, Purpose & Prosperity

## Overview

This podcast explores how business acumen meets biblical wisdom, featuring Phillip Anthony Mitchell, Tim Timberlake, and Toure Roberts at Investfest. The panel discusses prosperity theology, stewardship, character development, discernment of God's opportunities versus good opportunities, and maintaining intimacy with God amid success. The central thesis is that wealth and influence are tools for purpose, not ends in themselves, and that character and intimacy with God determine whether you can sustain the blessings you receive.

## When to Follow Podcast

- When building a business or ministry with biblical principles
- When discerning between multiple business or ministry opportunities
- When struggling with identity beyond your gifts or platform
- When wanting to advance spiritually and financially without compromising
- When learning to steward success and influence


## Discussed

### Topic 1: Discerning God Opportunities from Good Opportunities

#### Steps

##### Step 1: Test Every Opportunity Against Peace

When an opportunity arises, ask yourself whether it leaves you with sustained inner calm or growing anxiety. If it robs you of peace, it is too expensive regardless of how attractive it seems. A God opportunity brings lasting joy and purpose alignment, while a good opportunity only brings temporary happiness.

##### Step 2: Align with God-Given Purpose

Evaluate whether the opportunity aligns with your God-given purpose, not just your passions or desires. Passion and purpose are not the same - good opportunities may align with passion but not purpose.

##### Step 3: Evaluate Your Thirst Level

Ask yourself: Do I chase every opportunity that opens? Do I feel anxious when doors close? Do I compare my platform to others? If yes, you may be operating from desperation rather than discernment.

##### Step 4: Write Down Your Purpose

Spend time with God and write down what He has shown you about your purpose, calling, and vision. Use your written purpose as a filter for every opportunity.

##### Step 5: Establish Core Convictions

Before opportunities arise, establish biblical convictions that will govern your decisions. Write them down and let them determine your yeses and nos.

#### Examples

##### Example 1: Happiness vs. Joy

A good opportunity comes with happiness, but a God opportunity comes with joy. Even difficult, costly decisions that align with your purpose still carry God's peace.

##### Example 2: Thirsty People Walk Off Cliffs

Desperate, overly thirsty people walk through every open door and walk off cliffs. The devil dangles carrots in front of thirsty people.

##### Example 3: The $37 Million No

Tim Timberlake turned down a $37 million opportunity because it didn't align with core convictions. The deal would have cost reputation, witness, and required compromise.

#### Best Practices

- ✅ Test every opportunity against your peace
- ✅ Evaluate whether the opportunity aligns with your God-given purpose
- ✅ Reject opportunities that rob you of peace, even if they are attractive
- ✅ Cultivate intimacy with God as the foundation for discerning your calling
- ✅ Write down what God has shown you about your purpose
- ✅ Use your written purpose as a filter for every opportunity
- ✅ If God didn't show it to you, treat it as a no
- ✅ Establish convictions before opportunities arise
- ✅ Let your convictions determine your yeses and nos

#### Keep In Mind

- Passion and purpose are not the same - good opportunities may align with passion but not purpose
- It takes a strong person to say no to an open door
- You are not random - God knew you before He formed you in your mother's womb
- You will stand before God and give an account for every decision

#### Security & Safety Notes

- Good opportunities that pull you from your purpose are spiritually dangerous
- Thirsty people get led astray by every open door that appears
- Assuming God's silence is approval leads to stepping outside your purpose
- Compromising convictions for financial gain destroys your witness and character

#### Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Confusing passion for purpose
  **Solution:** Ask if the opportunity aligns with your God-given purpose, not just your desires
- **Problem:** Walking through every open door out of desperation
  **Solution:** Cultivate intimacy with God so you can discern which doors are yours
- **Problem:** Assuming silence from God means yes
  **Solution:** If it's not a clear yes from God, it's a no
- **Problem:** Saying yes to deals that violate core convictions
  **Solution:** Let established convictions protect you from compromising decisions

### Topic 2: Stewardship and Wealth Assignment

#### Steps

##### Step 1: Audit Current Stewardship

Take inventory of your current resources, time, talents, and finances. Assess how well you are stewarding what God has already placed in your hands.

##### Step 2: Seek Wisdom Over Wealth

Before praying for increased financial blessing, pray for wisdom to manage your current resources. Ask God to show you where you can be more faithful with what you have.

##### Step 3: Eliminate Comparison and Jealousy

Identify any jealousy or covetousness toward others' financial success. Redirect that energy toward focusing on your own calling and path.

##### Step 4: Remain Faithful at Your Current Level

Demonstrate faithfulness with current blessings. God will trust you with greater resources when you prove trustworthy with smaller ones.

##### Step 5: Understand Your Calling's Financial Demands

Understand the specific financial needs of your calling and compare them against your current resources. Don't compare your assignment to others.

##### Step 6: Trust God's Assignment

Release any desire for wealth that is not assigned to your purpose. Trust that God will provide exactly what you need for what He has called you to do.

#### Examples

##### Example 1: Biblical Prosperity

God blessed Abraham, David, and Solomon financially, showing scripture supports prosperity. There's nothing wrong with having wealth as long as it doesn't have you.

##### Example 2: Stewardship Principle

God will not trust you with greater responsibilities (a wedding cake) if He cannot trust you with smaller ones (a donut). You must first believe God for wisdom to steward current resources before seeking greater wealth.

##### Example 3: Parable of the Talents

Matthew 25 parable of talents - each person received according to their own ability. The potential is in both you (ability) and what God gives you (talent).

#### Best Practices

- ✅ Focus on stewarding current blessings before pursuing greater ones
- ✅ Believe God for wisdom to manage what you already have
- ✅ Avoid jealousy of others' financial blessings
- ✅ Stay focused on your own path and calling
- ✅ Show God you can handle current resources before believing for more
- ✅ Understand that your calling determines your financial assignment
- ✅ Trust that God will give you what you need for your specific purpose

#### Keep In Mind

- God is more concerned with your stewardship than with simply pouring out wealth on everyone
- God assigns wealth according to what He has called you to do

#### Security & Safety Notes

- Receiving a business does not guarantee keeping it without character and integrity
- Putting trust in riches rather than in God leads to spiritual danger

#### Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Being jealous of others' financial success
  **Solution:** Focus on your own path and trust God for what He has assigned to you
- **Problem:** Coveting financial blessings assigned to others
  **Solution:** Be content with what God has assigned to your purpose and calling

### Topic 3: Character Development and Advancement

#### Steps

##### Step 1: Embrace the School of Life

Stop resisting pain, hardships, trials, and tribulations. Recognize these as God's tools for shaping your character.

##### Step 2: Prioritize Character Over Gifts

Regularly evaluate whether you are developing character as much as you are developing your gifts and talents. Character sustains the success that gifts produce.

##### Step 3: Allow God to Shape You

Yield to God's process rather than rushing past difficulties. Trust that He is building the fortitude you need for future blessings.

##### Step 4: Identify Current Blessings to Sacrifice

List your current platforms, relationships, or blessings that may be holding you back from the next level. Ask God which ones need to be laid down.

##### Step 5: Lay It on the Altar

Place these blessings on the altar before God. Tell Him you trust Him with the outcome and you will not pick them back up until He tells you to.

##### Step 6: Trust the Delay

When blessings seem delayed, remind yourself that God delays to protect and develop you. Trust that delayed blessings are right on time.

#### Examples

##### Example 1: Character Outlasts Talent

Your talents can put you in a room, but your character can put you out of that room. Your gifts can put you on a mountaintop, but your character can take you off. Your gifts can give you relationships, but your character can ruin them.

##### Example 2: Release Former Levels

Before God advances you to a new level of influence, you must be willing to release the previous level completely. True sacrifice means laying down your current blessings, platforms, or relationships and not reclaiming them until God explicitly tells you.

##### Example 3: Delay Produces Right Timing

Every blessing received after a perceived delay was right on time. God's brilliance in not blessing you too quickly protects and develops you.

#### Best Practices

- ✅ Allow God to shape you through pain, hardships, trials, and tribulations
- ✅ Embrace the school of life rather than rushing past it
- ✅ Prioritize character development over rapid advancement
- ✅ Lay down current blessings, platforms, or relationships until God tells you to pick them back up
- ✅ Prove your heart by being willing to sacrifice what you love
- ✅ Die to your current level so that if God gives it back, you get to keep it
- ✅ Trust God's timing even when blessings seem delayed
- ✅ Learn to be content in the waiting
- ✅ Enjoy the view while waiting on God

#### Keep In Mind

- God uses life's difficulties to develop the character needed for sustained blessing
- The test of the heart is whether you can sacrifice what you've built
- Delayed blessings are often right on time and correct you in ways immediate blessings would not

#### Security & Safety Notes

- Skipping character development costs relationships, platforms, and influence
- Holding onto current levels prevents God from taking you to new levels
- Impatience during waiting seasons leads to poor decision-making

#### Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Trying to skip the development process
  **Solution:** Embrace trials as character-building opportunities designed by God
- **Problem:** Being unwilling to sacrifice current blessings for advancement
  **Solution:** Lay it on the altar and let it stay until you die to it
- **Problem:** Thinking delayed means denied
  **Solution:** Trust that God knows the end from the beginning and His timing is perfect

### Topic 4: Intimacy with God for Discernment

#### Steps

##### Step 1: Prioritize Private Time with God

Make time for God your first priority each day. This is not optional for those who want to steward influence well.

##### Step 2: Be Honest and Unsanitized

Talk to God honestly about your feelings, struggles, and desires. Don't hide behind empty religious clichés or sanitized theology when talking to God. Be raw and honest about your true feelings and struggles.

##### Step 3: Confess and Renounce Sin Regularly

Confess and renounce sin from your heart through honest prayer and repentance daily to maintain clean fellowship with God. Let Him search your heart.

##### Step 4: Open Up Fully to God

Don't hide your brokenness from God. Open up the deepest parts of yourself to Him, even the parts you don't understand.

##### Step 5: Yield Brokenness to God

Surrender your brokenness, pain, and imperfections to God. Trust that His wholeness can heal and restore you.

##### Step 6: Allow Transformation Through Intimacy

Let time with God change you. Like a married couple become one, intimacy with God should transform you into His image.

#### Examples

##### Example 1: Prayer Over Preaching

Prayer life is the strongest part of ministry, not preaching. Being honest with God about feelings and desires without sanitizing theology.

##### Example 2: Intimacy as Protection

Intimacy protects from making bad decisions and being led astray. The more intimate you are with God, the more discernment you have.

##### Example 3: Brokenness to Wholeness

Opening up brokenness to God's wholeness makes you whole. Worship is surrender - opening yourself to God and letting Him change you.

#### Best Practices

- ✅ Make God your first thought in the morning
- ✅ Have honest, unsanitized conversations with God
- ✅ Confess and renounce sin from your heart through honest prayer and repentance each day
- ✅ Yield your brokenness to God's wholeness
- ✅ Open up everything to God even when you don't understand it
- ✅ Trust God's holiness with your brokenness
- ✅ Cultivate intimacy with God as the foundation for discerning your calling
- ✅ Learn to say no - it's a powerful word that keeps you out of trouble
- ✅ Don't be so thirsty for success, money, or platforms

#### Keep In Mind

- Intimacy with God looks different for everyone - prayer room, walk in the park, etc.
- Intimacy with God transforms you into His image over time
- There is nothing that will satisfy you more in this life than a deep intimate personal relationship with Jesus that is happening in private

#### Security & Safety Notes

- Public religiosity without private intimacy leaves you vulnerable to bad decisions
- Trying to appear perfect before God blocks true intimacy and transformation
- Being overly thirsty for success, money, or platforms leads to being led astray

#### Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Thinking church attendance equals intimacy
  **Solution:** Develop a deep, private, honest relationship with God
- **Problem:** Not being honest with God about brokenness
  **Solution:** Open up fully and let God transform you through intimacy
- **Problem:** Being overly thirsty and chasing every open door
  **Solution:** Cultivate intimacy with God so you can discern which doors are yours

### Topic 5: Identity, Gifts, and Idolatry

#### Steps

##### Step 1: Ask Who You Are When Alone

Spend time alone asking, "Who am I when no one is watching? Who am I to me?" Don't define yourself by your work.

##### Step 2: Separate Gift from Identity

Recognize that your gift, platform, and work are not who you are. You are a son/daughter of God first.

##### Step 3: Evaluate Your Motives

Regularly examine whether you are using your gifts for self-promotion or for God's glory. Ask yourself who gets the attention.

##### Step 4: Think About the Recipient

Shift your focus from your idea or platform to the person who will be blessed by it. Think about the problem you are solving.

##### Step 5: Lay Your Business on the Altar

List your business, platform, influence, ideas, and desires. Ask God which ones need to be placed on the altar. Tell Him, "This all belongs to you. I'm fully committed to you."

##### Step 6: Let God Define You

Allow God to recognize you as His child rather than seeking identity from what you do or what others think of you.

#### Examples

##### Example 1: The Alone Question

Who are you when there's no one to talk to? Who are you to you? And if you only recognize yourself by what you do, then you're missing an opportunity to be recognized by the very person that God sees you as.

##### Example 2: Gift for Glory or God's Glory

You can use your gift for your glory or God's glory. Thinking about your business more than the person it blesses is like singing your own birthday song.

##### Example 3: The Idol Test

If you think about the gift more than the person who gave it to you, it may be an idol. If you think about the gift more than the person the gift is for, you're missing the point.

##### Example 4: Toure Roberts' Altar

Toure Roberts laid everything on the altar and trusted God with outcomes. If you try to control outcomes, you make yourself God in your own life.

#### Best Practices

- ✅ See yourself as a son/daughter first, separate from your gift
- ✅ Understand there's more value to you than what your hands are doing
- ✅ Let God recognize you rather than defining yourself by your work
- ✅ Evaluate your motives: do you think about the Giver, gift, and recipient in that order?
- ✅ Focus on the problem your idea solves, not just the idea itself
- ✅ Lay your business, platform, influence, and desires on the altar
- ✅ Trust God with outcomes instead of trying to control them
- ✅ Think about the Giver more than the gift
- ✅ Think about the recipient more than your idea
- ✅ Focus on solving problems rather than just making money

#### Keep In Mind

- God sees you as a son/daughter, not as what you do
- The gift is a means to serve others, not an end in itself
- God can trust someone whose business, platform, influence, ideas, and desires is on the altar
- Your gift is for the person it serves, not for your own promotion

#### Security & Safety Notes

- Basing identity on gifts or work leads to crisis when platforms fade
- Idolizing gifts hinders true blessings and fulfillment
- Trying to control outcomes makes you God in your own life and blocks true blessing
- Using gifts for self-promotion leads to idolatry

#### Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Recognizing yourself only by what you do
  **Solution:** Let God define your identity as His beloved child
- **Problem:** Thinking about what's in your hands more than what's in God's heart
  **Solution:** Refocus on God's heart for the people your gift is meant to serve
- **Problem:** Focusing on the gift rather than the Giver
  **Solution:** Shift your focus to what God wants to do through you for others
- **Problem:** Trying to control outcomes instead of trusting God
  **Solution:** Lay it on the altar and surrender control to God

### Topic 6: Contentment and Trusting God's Timing

#### Steps

##### Step 1: Learn Contentment in Every Season

Practice being content whether you are in abundance or lack. Make this a discipline in your life.

##### Step 2: Trust God's Timing in Delays

When waiting for breakthroughs, trust that God knows the end from the beginning. Delays are often for your development.

##### Step 3: Enjoy the Waiting Season

Don't just endure waiting - learn to enjoy the view. Let God teach you during the delay.

##### Step 4: Avoid Impulsive Decisions

When tempted to act impulsively because of delay or lack, remember that God's timing is perfect and rushing can cost you.

#### Examples

##### Example 1: Paul's Contentment

Apostle Paul learned contentment in both abundance and lack.

##### Example 2: Delay Develops Character

God delays blessings to develop character. Every blessing received after a perceived delay was right on time.

##### Example 3: Wrong Timing Is Harmful

The very thing prayed for would have been harmful if received at the wrong time. God's brilliance in not blessing you too quickly protects you.

#### Best Practices

- ✅ Trust God's timing even when blessings seem delayed
- ✅ Learn to enjoy the waiting season
- ✅ Be content in whatever state you find yourself
- ✅ Don't rush ahead of God's timing
- ✅ Slowly is the fastest way to get to where God desires for you to be

#### Keep In Mind

- Delayed blessings are often right on time and develop character you wouldn't gain otherwise
- The top of one mountain is the bottom of another - keep climbing

#### Security & Safety Notes

- Impatience during waiting seasons leads to poor decisions and missed growth
- The very thing prayed for would have been harmful if received at the wrong time

#### Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Thinking delayed means denied
  **Solution:** Trust God's brilliant timing and enjoy the process of waiting
- **Problem:** Making failure an identity
  **Solution:** Failure is an event, not an identity. The faster you lick your wounds and move on, the faster you'll be on track for your next assignment.
