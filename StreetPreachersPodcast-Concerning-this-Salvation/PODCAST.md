## Overview

This episode of the Street Preachers Podcast, hosted by Tim Timberlake and Phillip Anthony Mitchell, teaches from 1 Peter 1:10 about the exclusive nature of salvation through Jesus Christ. The hosts explain that there is only one salvation, one pathway to God, and that Jesus Christ is the singular way, truth, and life. They share personal testimonies of transformation from lives of crime and violence into ministry and leadership, and they urge listeners to not give up on those who have lost hope, because salvation is real and available through Christ.

## When to Follow Podcast

- When you need to understand that there is only one way to God through Jesus Christ and no other pathway
- When you are feeling hopeless, cannot see past tomorrow, or need encouragement in your faith
- When you want to learn how to engage community, resist false teaching, and live out a transformed Christian life

## Discussed

### The Exclusivity of Salvation Through Jesus Christ

#### Steps

##### Step 1: Understand that salvation is exclusive

There is only one salvation. The word "this" in "Concerning this salvation" is very important. There are no multiple salvations and no multiple roads to God. If there were multiple roads to God, then Jesus was a liar.

##### Step 2: Hear Jesus's own words

When Jesus was alive on earth he said, "I am the way, the truth, and the life. No one comes to the Father but through me." This statement is exclusive and narrow. Jesus is the singular way, the singular truth, and the singular life.

##### Step 3: Evaluate who is credible

You must choose: either Jesus was credible or he was a liar. If Jesus was credible, there is only one God and one pathway to God. No other founder of a major religion has ever come back from the grave. Jesus was seen alive by over 500 eyewitnesses who wrote accurate accounts that have been preserved in history.

##### Step 4: Know the cost of the apostles' witness

The apostles, with the exception of John, gave their lives as martyrs for what they saw. Peter was crucified upside down. James was thrown off the temple and his head bashed with a club. Another was flayed alive. One was thrust through with spears. They died for a resurrected Christ. Very rarely can you find a man to die for the truth, let alone die for a lie.

#### Examples

##### Example 1: Jesus's Exclusive Claim

Jesus said, "I am the way, the truth, and the life. No one comes to the Father but through me." Philip Anthony Mitchell did not make this statement. Tim Timberlake did not make this statement. Only Jesus made this claim.

##### Example 2: The Apostles' Martyrdom

Peter was crucified upside down. James was thrown off the temple and his head bashed with a club. Another apostle was flayed alive. One was thrust through with spears. They died for what they saw: a resurrected Jesus.

#### Best Practices

- ✅ Acknowledge that there is only one God and one pathway to God
- ✅ Examine the credibility of Jesus's resurrection with over 500 eyewitnesses
- ✅ Test all teaching against the exclusive claim of Jesus Christ
- ❌ Do not believe that there are multiple roads to God
- ❌ Do not place your favorite influencer or public figure above the claims of Jesus

#### Keep In Mind

### Evidence That Salvation Is Real and Transformative

#### Steps

##### Step 1: Consider the unity and prophecy of scripture

The Bible is the only religious book in the world with prophecies that have come to pass. Not one prophecy from the scriptures has ever failed. The 40 human authors wrote over 1500 years on three continents in three languages, yet there is a unity of thought from Genesis to Revelation that Phillip says can only come from the inspiration of the Holy Spirit.

##### Step 2: Reflect on the apostles' transformed lives

The apostles did not just write about the resurrection; they lived it and died for it. Peter, James, and others gave their lives as martyrs because they had seen the resurrected Christ with their own eyes.

##### Step 3: Look at transformed lives around you

Salvation is not just words. It is evidenced by real change in real people. Phillip Anthony Mitchell went from selling crack cocaine at age 12, to prison at 18, to being a minister with businesses. Tim Timberlake went from a life of pushing weight, shootouts, and violence in the streets of Queens and North Carolina to a preacher and podcast host.

##### Step 4: Recognize the work of the Holy Spirit

The Holy Spirit is alive on the inside of believers. Phillip says the spirit of God has attached himself to only one world religion, Christianity. The church is empowered by the Holy Spirit who united himself to the church on the day of Pentecost.

#### Examples

##### Example 1: Phillip Anthony Mitchell's Testimony

Phillip Anthony Mitchell graduated from North Carolina Central University two months after 9/11. He practiced another religion, stopped eating pork, prayed five times a day, but felt empty. He felt like something was missing. When he gave his life to Christ, he was suicidal in a bathroom over a toilet seat and met the King of Kings and the Lord of Lords who unzipped him and zipped him up with the Holy Spirit, transforming his entire life. That other religion did not do that.

##### Example 2: Tim Timberlake's Testimony

Tim Timberlake could not envision his life beyond 21. He was selling crack cocaine at 12, having sex with women and ruining their lives at 13, and robbed somebody at gunpoint at 17. He spent his 18th birthday in a prison cell at Riker's Island. He and Phillip ran the streets together, pushing weight, in shootouts, blanketing campuses with drugs. They never thought about tomorrow. They never talked about the future. They only thought about surviving the day they were in. Now he is a minister and host of the Street Preachers Podcast.

#### Best Practices

- ✅ Study the Bible and examine its prophecies for yourself
- ✅ Look at the transformed lives of believers as evidence of salvation
- ✅ Remember that the apostles died as martyrs for the resurrected Christ
- ✅ Recognize that the Holy Spirit is alive on the inside of every true believer
- ❌ Do not rely on artificial intelligence more than your own actual intelligence
- ❌ Do not accept empty platitudes or materialism as encouragement

#### Keep In Mind

- The prophets who prophesied about the grace that was to be yours searched and inquired carefully
- Salvation gives you a future, an eternity with God, and strength
- Phillip says the Holy Spirit has attached himself to only one world religion: Christianity
- Wherever the people of God are, power is there, because the Holy Spirit united himself to the church on the day of Pentecost

#### Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Relying on artificial intelligence, AI-generated content, or influencers instead of seeking truth for yourself. **Solution:** Use AI as a tool, but depend on your own actual intelligence and study the Bible
- **Problem:** Believing that your broken past disqualifies you from being used by God. **Solution:** God can use anyone. Neither Tim nor Phillip were supposed to be alive or free by all accounts, but God transformed them

### Applying Salvation: Rest, Community, and Hope

#### Steps

##### Step 1: Understand that rest is holy and godly

God created everything in six days and on the seventh day he rested. God did not rest because he was tired. He rested to teach us a lesson and to give us a model that we need to know how to work and rest. Rest is godly. Having hustle to the point where you kill yourself is not godly. Vacations matter. Pulling away matters.

##### Step 2: Follow the example of Jesus in rest

Jesus himself would slip away often to be alone with God. We see Jesus preaching and slipping away, teaching and slipping away, feeding and slipping away, doing ministry and then slipping away.

##### Step 3: Do not give up on people who have lost hope

If you have a teenager that is wild, reckless, or far away, do not give up on them. Do not let them go. There is a God in heaven who loves and who cares, who can transform lives and pull men out of anything. Belief and prayer are powerful.

##### Step 4: Engage in community and network intentionally

You must go out of your inner circle to get into a new circle and build a new community. Sometimes your brothers, sisters, aunts, uncles, and co-workers are not where you are yet, and that is okay. You have to leave people where they are and go walk in your own greatness. The person next to you at an event might be your next business partner or prayer partner.

#### Examples

##### Example 1: Taking a Break Was Called Rest

Tim and Phillip took a break between season one and season two of the Street Preachers Podcast. People speculated there was beef between them, but a pause is called rest. Rest is holy. Rest is godly. They came back rested and ready for season two.

##### Example 2: The Street Team Community

The Street Team is the collective family all over the world that is helping to spread the gospel of Jesus Christ. On tour, those nights are impactful encounters, not just two guys sitting on seats with a mic. Tears are shed, breakthroughs happen, and testimonies of healing emerge.

##### Example 3: Investfest as a Gathering for Growth

Investfest is a financial literacy festival that drew 25,000 people per day in 2025 and is projected to reach 30,000 people per day in 2026. It brings together investors, business professionals, and entrepreneurs for education and execution. The information is on the hosts, but the execution is on you. It is a place to network, fellowship, and meet the person next to you who may have the same mindset.

#### Best Practices

- ✅ Take rest seriously. Rest is holy and godly, not a sign of weakness
- ✅ Follow the model of Jesus, who slipped away often to be alone with God
- ✅ Do not give up on people, especially teenagers, who seem hopeless or far from God
- ✅ Pray and believe for those who cannot see past tomorrow
- ✅ Engage in community by attending events, talking to people next to you, and building new circles
- ✅ Network at events such as Investfest and Street Preachers tour stops
- ❌ Do not buy into narratives or rumors about beef between the hosts or other believers
- ❌ Do not overwork yourself to the point of death in the name of hustle
- ❌ Do not ignore the person next to you at an event

#### Keep In Mind

- God created everything in six days and rested on the seventh day to give us a model
- Rest is holy and godly
- Jesus slipped away often to be alone with God despite having crowds
- The prepared messenger is always better than the prepared message
- The information is on the podcast hosts; the execution is on you
- People in your inner circle may not share your ambitions or goals; that is okay
- The person next to you at an event is there for the same reason you are there

#### Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Believing that taking a break or resting means you are unfaithful or that there is "beef" between believers. **Solution:** Understand that rest is holy, godly, and biblical. God rested. Jesus rested. Taking a break is not a sign of conflict.
- **Problem:** Being so focused on the stage or meet-and-greets that you ignore the person next to you. **Solution:** Look to the left and right of you at events. Network. Fellowship. The person next to you may be your next business partner or prayer partner.
- **Problem:** Believing that a teenager or loved one who is lost or wild is beyond hope. **Solution:** Do not give up on them. Pray, believe, and remember. God can transform lives no matter how broken they are.
- **Problem:** Thinking that you cannot see past tomorrow or that your future is hopeless. **Solution:** This salvation gives you a future and an eternity with God. Salvation has strength and hope for tomorrow.
