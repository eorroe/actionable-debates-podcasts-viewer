# This Is Temporary ｜ Street Preachers Podcast ｜ Phillip Anthony Mitchell & Tim Timberlake

## Overview

The podcast teaches that life's difficulties are temporary and therefore tolerable, and that true joy is found in Christ alone rather than in movable things. The podcast builds a community called the "street team" committed to spreading the gospel of Jesus Christ through conversation rooted in Scripture.

## When to Follow Podcast

- When you are going through trials, tribulations, or persecution and need biblical encouragement
- When you feel distant from God and need practical steps to reconnect through worship
- When you want to build a community of believers on mission for Jesus Christ
- When you need to remember that Christ must become enough for you regardless of your circumstances


## Discussed

### Topic 1: Understanding That Life's Hardships Are Temporary

#### Steps

##### Step 1: Identify the hardship you are currently facing

Name the trial, disappointment, or pain you are experiencing. Write it down so you can clearly see what you are dealing with rather than letting it remain vague.

##### Step 2: Declare that your hardship is temporary

Speak out loud: "This is temporary." Apply this truth to your situation by saying, "I'm going through this hardship. Temporary. I'm going through this trial. Temporary."

##### Step 3: Anchor yourself to the eternal promise

Meditate on 1 Peter 1:3-5, which describes your inheritance as imperishable, undefiled, and unfading, kept for you in heaven. Remember that your faith is guarded through trials for a salvation ready to be revealed in the last days, as 1 Peter 1:5 describes.

#### Examples

##### Example 1: Financial loss

If you lost your job or experienced a financial setback, speak over the situation: "This job loss is temporary. My provision comes from the Lord." Anchor to the promise that God will supply your needs according to His riches in glory.

##### Example 2: Relationship pain

If you are grieving the loss of a relationship or dealing with family conflict, declare: "This pain is temporary." Remember that earthly relationships will fade, but your position in Christ is eternal.

#### Best Practices

- Speak out loud rather than keeping it in your mind
- ✅ Write down hardships to make them tangible
- Meditate on Scripture, especially passages about eternal life and heaven
- ❌ Do not minimize your pain by saying it doesn't matter
- ❌ Do not rush past the grief without acknowledging it

#### Keep In Mind

- Peter called life "a little while" and "temporary" to encourage believers enduring trials.
- In 1 Peter 1:7, trials test the genuineness of faith.
- Trials test faith, which the speakers described as more precious than gold that perishes.

#### Security & Safety Notes

- Do not suppress your emotions or pretend you are not hurting. Acknowledge the pain while simultaneously declaring the truth about its temporary nature.
- If you are experiencing depression or thoughts of self-harm, seek immediate professional help and speak with a trusted pastor or counselor.

#### Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** You feel like your hardship will never end.
  **Solution:** Remind yourself daily that everything in this life is temporary. Set a timer on your phone to speak this truth three times per day until it becomes your default belief.

- **Problem:** You compare your suffering to others and feel yours is worse or invalid.
  **Solution:** Remember that all believers face challenges on their level. Your pain is real, and the principle that it is temporary applies regardless of the magnitude of your trial.

### Topic 2: Practicing Consistent Rejoicing Regardless of Circumstances

#### Steps

##### Step 1: Understand what biblical rejoicing means

Rejoice means to intentionally choose joy and praise God again, even when circumstances are difficult. It is a repeated choice to remember that God is good and present with you, not a feeling based on your current circumstances.

##### Step 2: Choose to rejoice when you do not feel like it

When you are in pain, when you are disappointed, when you are weary, speak out loud: "I will bless the Lord at all times, and His praise shall continually be in my mouth." This is what David declared in Psalm 34:1.

##### Step 3: Rejoice in specific moments throughout your day

Rejoice when you wake up. Rejoice when you go to work. Rejoice when you are in hardship. Rejoice when things are going well. Rejoice when you do not have words to say. The repetition trains your flesh that the Spirit of God leads you, not your emotions.

##### Step 4: Rejoice because of who God is, not what is happening to you

Your rejoicing is based on the truth of who God says He is, not on your changing circumstances. Rejoice because the Savior of all who believe is with you in the middle of your trial.

#### Examples

##### Example 1: Rejoicing during persecution

Peter wrote to believers facing persecution under Nero, losing their lives, families, and property. He told them to rejoice because their Savior was with them in the middle of their suffering. Apply this truth when you face opposition for your faith.

##### Example 2: Rejoicing when you feel empty

When you have no words to pray and no feelings of God's presence, rejoice anyway. Say: "Lord, I do not feel like rejoicing, but I choose to rejoice because You are with me." This act of obedience shifts your focus from your flesh to the Spirit.

#### Best Practices

- ✅ Set reminders to rejoice intentionally
- ✅ Rejoice out loud so your ears hear the truth being spoken
- ✅ Use Scripture as your foundation for rejoicing, not your feelings
- ❌ Do not wait until you feel like rejoicing to do it
- ❌ Do not treat happiness and rejoicing as the same. Happiness depends on circumstances; rejoicing depends on the truth of who God is, the speakers said.

#### Keep In Mind

- Rejoicing is not a denial of pain. It is a declaration of God's presence in the middle of pain, according to the speakers.
- Rejoicing refocuses attention away from worldly distractions and toward God's truth.
- The voice you listen to shapes the direction of your life. Listen to the voice of God.

#### Security & Safety Notes

- Do not use rejoicing as a way to suppress genuine grief or trauma. Rejoice while also processing your emotions with a trusted pastor or counselor.
- If you are in an abusive situation, rejoice in God's presence while also taking practical steps to protect yourself and get help.

#### Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** You only rejoice when circumstances are favorable.
  **Solution:** Practice rejoicing during small disappointments so that when trials come, rejoicing is already a habit.

- **Problem:** You believe rejoicing means you are pretending your pain is not real.
  **Solution:** Rejoicing is not denying reality. It is choosing to focus on a greater reality: God is with you. You can feel pain and still choose to rejoice, the speakers said.

### Topic 3: Making Christ the Source of Your Joy

#### Steps

##### Step 1: Evaluate where your joy currently comes from

Ask yourself honestly: If I lost my job, my spouse, my health, my ministry, or my possessions, would I still have joy? If the answer is no, your joy is attached to things that can be lost rather than to Christ.

##### Step 2: Decide that Christ alone must be enough

Make a conscious decision that Christ is the source of your joy. Say out loud: "Christ is enough for me. I do not need anything else to experience joy." Repeat this decision each time circumstances challenge your joy.

##### Step 3: Practice gratitude for Christ first, then for everything else

When Christ is the foundation of your joy and everything else is secondary, your heart will overflow with gratitude for the blessings in your life. But if Christ is not the foundation of your joy, losing blessings will be devastating, the speakers said.

##### Step 4: Test your joy when loss comes

When you experience loss, your reaction will reveal whether Christ is truly your source of joy. If you can still worship, still trust, still rejoice in the middle of loss, then Christ is your foundation.

#### Examples

##### Example 1: Losing a job

If you lose your job and you have placed your joy in Christ, you will grieve the loss but you will not be destroyed. You will say: "I lost my job, but I still have Jesus. He is my provider, and He is enough."

##### Example 2: Losing a loved one

When you go to a funeral and lose someone you love, if Christ is your source of joy, you will mourn with hope. You will remember that your loved one is with the Lord and that you will see them again. Your grief is real, but your joy in Christ is unshakable.

#### Best Practices

- ✅ Practice the presence of God through prayer and worship so that Christ becomes your primary source of joy
- ✅ Keep a gratitude journal focused specifically on who Christ is, not just what He has done for you
- ❌ Do not wait for a crisis to discover whether Christ is enough for you
- ❌ Do not pursue additional opportunities, wealth, or improved relationships as the source of your joy

#### Keep In Mind

- The speakers described Christ as a constant in your life and everything else as movable and fading.
- Choosing to root joy in Christ means it is not dependent on changing circumstances.
- Finding joy in Christ can sustain a believer through trials, in community, and in service, the speakers said.

#### Security & Safety Notes

- Do not neglect practical needs while pursuing spiritual joy. God provides for both your spiritual and physical needs. If you are struggling to meet basic needs, seek help from your church community and take practical steps to address the situation.

#### Common Pitfalls

  - **Problem:** You believe you need additional blessings such as a larger ministry, more wealth, or improved relationships before Christ is enough for you.
   **Solution:** More success or recognition will not produce lasting satisfaction, the speakers said. The speakers described Christ as the only one who can satisfy the human need for purpose and connection. Start today by spending time with Him and choosing to delight in Him alone.

- **Problem:** You feel guilty for enjoying good things like your spouse, children, or career.
  **Solution:** You can and should be thankful for these blessings. The problem only arises when these blessings become the foundation of your joy. Enjoy the gift, but keep your joy rooted in the Giver.

### Topic 4: Worshiping When God Feels Distant

#### Steps

##### Step 1: Recognize that God's silence does not mean He is absent

There are seasons where God feels close and seasons where He feels distant. The seasons of listening to God's voice do not mean that He is not speaking or that He is not present.

##### Step 2: Choose to worship anyway

When you do not feel God's presence, when prayer feels empty, when worship feels like going through the motions, choose to worship anyway. Worship involves your heart, not just a song you sing.

##### Step 3: Engage your body in worship

Take a walk and worship. Sing praises even when you do not feel like it. Spend time in your prayer room. Draw near to God through intentional worship. When you worship, you draw near to Him.

##### Step 4: Allow worship to reveal patterns or attitudes you need to let go of.

Every time you worship, God calls you to surrender behaviors that hinder your relationship with Him. Worship is not just about music. It is about surrendering habits and attitudes so that Christ can live more fully in you.

#### Examples

##### Example 1: Abraham and Isaac

Abraham took Isaac up the mountain to worship. He had to manage the fire, the wood, and the knife until God provided the ram. Burdens that build character you can carry with you, but burdens that are too heavy you must surrender to God until God provides relief. Worship is the space where you bring these things to God, the speakers said.

##### Example 2: Walking and worshiping

When God feels distant, go for a walk and worship. Do not wait for a specific emotion. Make yourself close to Him through intentional worship. The act of worship itself draws you near to God.

#### Best Practices

- ✅ Worship, not just on Sundays or when you feel like it
- ✅ Engage your body in worship: stand, kneel, walk, sing, or sit in silence
- ✅ Use worship to refocus your attention from yourself to God
- ❌ Do not wait for emotions to lead you into worship
- ❌ Do not reduce worship to music alone. Worship involves surrender, the speakers said.

#### Keep In Mind

- Worship is a practice you engage in, not something God does for you. He created you to worship, not because He needs it, but because you need it, the speakers said.
- When you worship, you feel connected to God because He designed you for that connection, the speakers said. You may feel uncertain or anxious without God's presence guiding your life.
- Abraham had no band, no keyboard, no worship team, and no speakers. He had wood, rope, fire, and a blade. Worship involves surrender, not production, the speakers said.

#### Security & Safety Notes

- Do not rely solely on emotional experiences during worship. True worship is obedience and surrender, not a feeling. If you consistently feel nothing during worship, speak with a pastor or spiritual mentor to ensure you are not experiencing spiritual dryness or depression that needs attention.

#### Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** You only worship when you feel close to God.
  **Solution:** Worship is a discipline. Choose to worship when you feel distant. The act of worship itself will draw you near to God, the speakers said.

- **Problem:** You believe worship is only singing songs on Sunday morning.
  **Solution:** Expand your understanding of worship. Worship is how you live your life, the speakers said. Every moment can be an act of worship when you surrender everything to God.

### Topic 5: Demonstrating Love Through Sacrificial Action, Not Just Words

#### Steps

##### Step 1: Examine whether your love for Christ is just words or a demonstration

Ask yourself: Is my love for Jesus just words, or does it show up in my life through self-sacrificial action? Real love involves what you are willing to give up.

##### Step 2: Identify what needs to die in you for Christ to be magnified

What habits, desires, or attachments are keeping you from loving Christ? Be honest before God about what He is asking you to surrender. Worship is the space where you bring these things to God.

##### Step 3: Live self-sacrificially for Christ

Let your life reflect the love Christ demonstrated on the cross. Surrender your ambitions, preferences, and comfort so that Christ can be made known through your life.

##### Step 4: Let the cross remind you that you are loved

When you shed tears, remember the cross: "I am loved." When you go through hardship, remember the cross: "I am loved." When your prayers go unanswered, remember the cross: "I am loved." The cross is the ultimate demonstration that you are loved, and nothing can take that away from you, the speakers said.

#### Examples

##### Example 1: Wearing two crosses

Phillip Anthony Mitchell wears two crosses around his neck: one representing the cross Christ carried, and one representing the cross he carries. This is a sign of love. It reminds him daily that he must carry his cross because Christ carried His.

##### Example 2: Loving when it costs you

If you love Christ, you will be willing to lose your job, your reputation, or your comfort for His sake. This is not suicidal. It is sacrificial. It is the same love that caused Christ to die for you while you were still a sinner.

#### Best Practices

- ✅ Let your actions speak louder than your words. Say less and do more for Christ.
- ✅ Meditate on the cross daily so that you never forget how much you are loved
- ❌ Do not say "I love Jesus" while living a life that contradicts that claim
- ❌ Do not attach God's love to material blessings or favorable circumstances

#### Keep In Mind

- Love involves action, not only emotion, the speakers said.
- Paul wrote to the church in Rome: "While we were yet sinners, Christ died for us." The speakers described this as the evidence of God's love for you.
- Nothing can take away the meaning of the cross. No loss, no disappointment, no pain can erase the fact that Christ died for you, the speakers said.

#### Security & Safety Notes

- Do not confuse sacrificial love with self-harm. God calls you to live a life of purpose and stewardship. Sacrificial love means surrendering your own desires for the sake of Christ, not harming yourself or neglecting your responsibilities, the speakers said.

#### Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** You attach God's love to blessings like a good job, a good spouse, or good health.
  **Solution:** Separate God's love from His blessings. He loves you because of the cross, not because of what He gives you. When you lose blessings, you still have His love, the speakers said.

- **Problem:** You say you love Jesus but you are unwilling to give up anything for Him.
  **Solution:** Ask the Holy Spirit to reveal what is keeping you from loving Christ. Be willing to surrender it. Love involves sacrifice, the speakers said.

### Topic 6: Building a Community on Mission for Jesus Christ

#### Steps

##### Step 1: Understand what the street team is

The street team is a community of believers who are on mission for the Lord Jesus Christ. Members live out this mission in their own areas of influence, including their family, workplace, and local community.

##### Step 2: Engage with the community

Comment on episodes. Share what is on your heart in the comments. Share testimonies. Encourage other believers in the comments. Have conversations. Love one another. The street team is a community on mission for Jesus.

##### Step 3: Share the content with others

Share episodes with your friends, your family, and anyone else who has not yet heard the gospel. Help continue the spread of the gospel of Jesus Christ through this media and platform.

##### Step 4: Carry the message into your sphere of influence

Use your words and your Christian testimony to share the gospel. You do not need a bullhorn or a platform. You just need to be willing to represent Christ in your everyday life.

#### Examples

##### Example 1: Inviting a neighbor to church

You hear something in an episode that encourages you. Share the message with a neighbor who is going through a hard time. Send them the link. Invite them to your local church. That is part of the street team.

##### Example 2: Encouraging someone in the comments

You watch an episode and the Lord gives you a word for someone else. Write it in the comments. It might not be for Tim and Phil. It might be for someone else who is watching and needs encouragement. That is community.

#### Best Practices

- ✅ Comment, like, subscribe, and share to help the message spread
- ✅ Encourage other believers in the comments with testimonies and words of encouragement
- ❌ Do not use the comments as a place for criticism or negativity
- ❌ Do not consume content without sharing it with someone who needs it

#### Keep In Mind

- The street team is not just the hosts. The street team is anyone on mission for Christ, representing Him wherever they are.
- The gospel is needed today. Your witness and your life matter, the speakers said.
- Comments and engagement encourage the hosts. As human beings, they need encouragement too. Your words carry weight.

#### Security & Safety Notes

- Do not share personal information in public comments. Keep your engagement encouraging and appropriate.
- If you are in your local community where sharing the gospel is dangerous, share the gospel discreetly and avoid unnecessary risk. The speakers said that both boldness and prudence have their place.

#### Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** You think the street team is only for the hosts or for people on a platform.
  **Solution:** The street team is anyone on mission for Christ. If you represent Christ, you are a street preacher, the speakers said. Your areas of influence, including your family, friends, and local community, are your mission field.

- **Problem:** You consume content without engaging or sharing.
  **Solution:** The hosts rely on engagement to continue spreading the gospel. Comment, share, and invite others. Your engagement helps another person in need find Christ.
