# Winning the War Within w/ Pastor Philip Anthony Mitchell

## Overview

This podcast episode from "Girls Gone Bible" features an intimate interview with Pastor Philip Anthony Mitchell discussing the urgency of end-time stewardship, maintaining spiritual fire, suffering well, preaching amid personal sickness, and the spiritual dangers of pride. The conversation explores how believers can live faithfully for Christ, probably in the last seconds of the church age, contend inwardly against shame and depression, and steward God's favor without idolizing platform or fame. Pastor Mitchell shares two promises he made to God, his theology of suffering as a gift, and his commitment to faithful proclamation even when sick in his own body.

## When to Follow Podcast

- When preparing to minister or preach and need a framework for urgency and faithfulness
- When walking through seasons of suffering, depression, or spiritual dryness and need biblical permission to feel
- When wrestling with pride or the temptation to idolize platform, influence, or personal fame
- When discerning how to respond to critique, slander, or spiritual attack on your ministry
- When seeking accountability structures and godly relationships that keep you grounded

## Discussed

### Topic 1: Urgency of the Times / End-Time Stewardship

#### Steps

##### Step 1: Assess the Urgency of the Moment

Before stepping onto any platform, Sunday gathering, podcast, or conference, ask yourself whether this could be the last time you proclaim the gospel in that room. Recognize that every moment of ministry is life or death and treat it accordingly.

##### Step 2: Evaluate Your Proclamation

When any given Sunday, podcast, or conference ends, ask: Was Christ exalted? Did we have gospel conversations? Were the scriptures properly proclaimed and faithfully exegeted rather than isogeted? Let these questions govern your preparation.

##### Step 3: Take the Call to Multiply Disciples Seriously

Do not compartmentalize your faith. Take your walk with Christ seriously and take your call to spread the gospel and multiply disciples seriously. Get out into the highways and byways making Jesus known and compelling people to come into the kingdom.

##### Step 4: Reject Complacency

If you are not about advancing the gospel and being with God's people, reconsider what you are doing. The hour is now to be serious about the work and purposes of the kingdom. Complacency in this era is a failure of stewardship.

#### Examples

##### Example 1: The Last Sunday

Pastor Philip describes never knowing what Sunday will be his last, what podcast will be his last, or what conference will be his last. There have been testimonies of people leaving a gathering and not making it home, including a brother in their church who died on a motorcycle after leaving a gathering and a young man who lost his life the week he planned to attend a 2019 gathering.

##### Example 2: The Front Line of Battle

Every moment of speaking, whether on tour or in a Sunday gathering, feels like being on the front line of warfare. There is real joy off the platform, but on the platform it is life or death.

##### Example 3: The End-Time Burden

Pastor Philip describes the burden to extend the Girls Gone Bible tour as more than a podcast — they want to be with God's people, teach, preach, lay hands on the sick, look people in the eye when they are shedding tears, and get the gospel to God's people.

#### Best Practices

- ✅ Treat every Sunday, podcast, and conference as potentially your last
- ✅ Ask whether Christ was exalted and whether scriptures were properly proclaimed
- ✅ Take your call to spread the gospel with urgency
- ✅ Go into the highways and byways making Jesus known
- ✅ Compel people to come into the kingdom
- ✅ Do not compartmentalize your faith from your daily life

#### Keep In Mind

- Life is fragile and held in the palm of God's hands
- No one knows what day will be their last
- The Holy Spirit is sounding an alarm in our nation and around the world
- Now is the hour to be serious about the work and purposes of the kingdom

#### Security & Safety Notes

- Treating ministry as casual or routine when it is life or death is spiritually dangerous
- Assuming you have more time than you actually do leads to complacency and unfaithful stewardship
- Playing around with the gospel when lives hang in the balance is not an option

#### Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Treating ministry as casual or routine
  **Solution:** Remember every moment could be someone's last opportunity to hear the gospel
- **Problem:** Isogeting scripture to suit personal preferences
  **Solution:** Ensure faithful exegesis and fidelity to the text in every proclamation
- **Problem:** Being more concerned with platform than people
  **Solution:** Focus on the person in front of you, not the size of the crowd

### Topic 2: Keeping the Fire / Intimacy with Christ

#### Steps

##### Step 1: Normalize Seasons of Drift

Understand that every believer goes through seasons where their fire burns dim or they feel cold and distant from God. This is normal in our humanity. Do not condemn yourself for it.

##### Step 2: Return to the Source

When your fire has grown cold, return to the source: Jesus himself. Go back to the secret place and be honest. Say, "Lord, I am in a dry place. I have drifted away from you." Honesty in the secret place is the starting point for rekindling.

##### Step 3: Surround Yourself with Fiery Believers

Get around community who is on fire. Tell them you have drifted and ask them to pray for you. Put yourself in gatherings where people are on fire.

##### Step 4: Throw Wood on the Fire

Do what is necessary to reignite: community, scripture, prayer, fasting, purity. Acknowledge when you have fallen from God and take intentional action to throw wood back on the fire.

##### Step 5: Pursue Holiness and Purity

The more you read, pray, consecrate, fast, distance yourself from iniquity and sin, and pursue holiness and purity of heart, the more you will grow on fire. Intimacy with Christ is the fire.

#### Examples

##### Example 1: The Quiet Fire

Fire is not about platform or fame. There is a fire that is voracious and loud, but there is also a quiet fire of an old woman who sits in the back of a church and nobody knows her name. She does not have a global platform or podcast, but when she kneels to pray, demons tremble. When she goes into a prayer closet, she shifts atmospheres.

##### Example 2: The Donkey Illustration

We are just the donkeys that Jesus is riding on. The people are screaming "Hosanna!" and laying branches on the floor for the person on top of the donkey, not for the donkey. If the donkey could talk in its prideful state, it would say, "Look at all these people throwing branches for me." We must not take the glory that belongs to Christ alone.

##### Example 3: Pentecostal Fire

When the Holy Spirit was poured out on 120 disciples on the day of Pentecost, the first thing the Holy Spirit touched was their mouths, which are connected to the heart. God set those 120 on fire. It is God's will for His people to be on fire, which means being very intimate with Him.

#### Best Practices

- ✅ Normalize seasons of drift and do not condemn yourself
- ✅ Return to the secret place and be honest with God
- ✅ Surround yourself with community who is on fire
- ✅ Engage in prayer, fasting, scripture, and purity
- ✅ Pursue intimacy with Christ rather than platform
- ✅ Understand fire as symbolic of closeness to Christ

#### Keep In Mind

- God desires for His people to be on fire, not lukewarm or distant
- A quiet fire in an obscure believer is powerful
- It is not about a platform and a name; it is about how intimate you are with Christ
- If God is a consuming fire, intimacy with Him is what ignites your own fire

#### Security & Safety Notes

- The enemy can make you feel condemned for your feelings and make you think God is mad at you
- Condemning yourself for drifting distances you further from God
- Chasing platform and fame instead of Christ leads to a counterfeit fire
- Public religiosity without private intimacy leaves you vulnerable to losing your fire

#### Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Feeling condemned when your fire grows cold
  **Solution:** Normalize drift and return honestly to the secret place
- **Problem:** Defining fire by platform size or fame
  **Solution:** Understand fire as intimacy with Christ, not visibility or volume
- **Problem:** Trying to reignite without community
  **Solution:** Get around community who is on fire. Tell them you have drifted and ask them to pray for you.

### Topic 3: Suffering Well / Theology of Suffering

#### Steps

##### Step 1: Normalize Suffering and Pain

Recognize that suffering and pain, and the distance from God they sometimes create, are normal. Do not allow the enemy to make you feel condemned for your feelings. God gives us grace to feel and has given us emotions to feel.

##### Step 2: Develop a Better Theology of Suffering

Stop treating suffering like failure or a disease. The West often hides behind veneers of false perfection, but this does people a disservice. Embrace suffering as part of the Christian experience. There is no walk with Jesus without suffering.

##### Step 3: Learn to Suffer for Righteousness

When suffering comes because you are in the will of God, you can whisper in your brokenness, "Lord, thank you that you have allowed me to suffer with you and to identify with your suffering." Suffering for righteousness unites you with Christ and shapes your character.

##### Step 4: Extract Good from Suffering

Allow suffering to strengthen your faith, shape your character, and identify you with Christ. Learn to wipe tears from your eyes while thanking Him at the same time. Extract all the good things that come out of suffering.

##### Step 5: Recognize Suffering as a Gift

Some suffering is a gift from God. The Apostle Paul connected suffering and being granted in the same sentence. Ask, "God, what are you trying to do in me as you are allowing me to go through this suffering?"

##### Step 6: Allow Yourself to Feel

Read the Psalms. They give you permission to feel, to be broken, to cry, to weep, to mourn, and to even question God. The Psalms were written by men inspired by the Holy Spirit and contain the full array of human emotion.

#### Examples

##### Example 1: The Psalms Give Permission

The Psalms, the largest book preserved in the scriptures, show everything from radical joy to radical sadness. They give us permission to be humans who God understands. We were made from dust and our frailties.

##### Example 2: Suffering Shapes Character

Suffering is part of the Christian experience. When suffering for righteousness, we are brought closer to Christ, our character is shaped, and our faith is strengthened. There is beauty in suffering that unites us with Jesus in such a way that just being on mountaintops will never do.

##### Example 3: Paul's Example

The Apostle Paul wrote from a Roman prison cell that suffering is a gift granted to us from God. Even while suffering in his body, from social persecution, and from death threats, he recognized that God does not completely remove suffering but is gracious in it, with fruit and lessons being learned.

#### Best Practices

- ✅ Normalize suffering and pain rather than treating them as failures
- ✅ Allow yourself to feel without condemnation
- ✅ Read the Psalms to find permission for your emotions
- ✅ Suffer well, not just live well
- ✅ Extract good from suffering
- ✅ Ask God what He is doing in you through suffering
- ✅ Remember that God allows suffering for your good and His glory
- ✅ Do not hide behind a veneer of false perfection

#### Keep In Mind

- Suffering is part of the Christian experience
- There is no walk with Jesus without suffering
- God will never create a life for us that makes Him unnecessary
- Even your favorite preacher or a platform cannot remove suffering from your life
- The enemy wants to make you feel condemned for your feelings and make you think God is mad at you

#### Security & Safety Notes

- Hiding suffering and pretending to be fine damages the body of Christ
- Treating suffering as failure leads to isolation and despair
- Condemning yourself for feeling distant from God pushes you further from Him
- False perfection on social media robs people of hope that even heroes suffer

#### Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Treating suffering as a sign of failed faith
  **Solution:** Understand suffering as part of the Christian experience and a tool for growth
- **Problem:** Condemning yourself for feeling distant from God
  **Solution:** Read the Psalms and remember God gives you grace to feel
- **Problem:** Hiding behind a veneer of perfection
  **Solution:** Pull back the curtain and let people see your humanity
- **Problem:** Believing suffering means God is absent
  **Solution:** God walks with you through the valley and shapes you in the process

### Topic 4: Preaching While Sick / Faith Amid Sickness

#### Steps

##### Step 1: Acknowledge Your Condition Honestly

Pastor Philip deals with a medical condition and is seeing a specialist. He posted about being laid up in a medical bed so people could see that his life is not perfect and that he is suffering with the brothers and sisters.

##### Step 2: Separate Your Condition from God's Character

Understand that your sickness does not change God's character, nature, or the truth about God's word. There is nothing you are going through that changes the truth of scripture or who God is.

##### Step 3: Preach What Is Biblical Regardless of Your State

You can preach healing because it is a biblical thing to preach. You can preach healing because Christ is a healer. Even while carrying sickness in your own body, you can still preach because your sickness does not negate the biblical mandate or God's ability to heal.

##### Step 4: Believe for Your Own Healing While Ministering to Others

As you lay hands on a sister or brother, lay hands on yourself. Do not let your medical condition hinder you from doing God's work or from believing for healing for other people. Pastor Philip had 100% faith that God was healing people in the room while he was sick in his own body.

##### Step 5: Preach from Scripture, Not from Sensation

Preach healing because the scripture teaches that signs and wonders will follow those who believe, and they will lay hands on the sick and the sick shall recover. The prayers of the righteous avail much. Let scripture be your foundation, not your physical condition.

#### Examples

##### Example 1: Preaching Healing While Sick

The Sunday after being in a medical bed, Pastor Philip preached Acts chapter 2 on the coming of the Spirit. During the altar call for healing, while he was sick in his own body, he believed God with 100% certainty that God was healing physical bodies, minds battling mental trauma, and hearts battling emotional trauma. There was not even an ounce of doubt in his heart.

##### Example 2: Doctrinal Foundation Over Physical Feeling

Pastor Philip states, "I don't have to be healed to preach healing." My sickness does not change His character, nature, or word. This is not about feelings; it is about who Christ is and what the scriptures teach.

##### Example 3: Laying Hands on Yourself

While laying hands on others for healing, Pastor Philip also lays hands on himself. His temporary circumstance does not negate the goodness of Christ, His character, His nature, or His word. He believes he is healed in Jesus' name and is not worried.

#### Best Practices

- ✅ Be honest about your medical condition rather than hiding it
- ✅ Do not let your sickness become an excuse to stop ministering
- ✅ Separate your condition from God's unchanging character and word
- ✅ Preach what is biblical regardless of your physical state
- ✅ Believe for your own healing while believing for others
- ✅ Lay hands on others while also laying hands on yourself
- ✅ Anchor your faith in scripture, not in physical sensation

#### Keep In Mind

- Your sickness does not change God's character, nature, or word
- There is nothing you are going through that changes the truth of scripture
- Christ is a healer regardless of your condition
- Your temporary circumstance does not negate the goodness of Christ

#### Security & Safety Notes

- Allowing sickness to stop your ministry hinders the gospel
- Letting doubt creep in because of your condition undermines your faith and the faith of those you minister to
- Hiding your condition creates a false veneer that damages authenticity and trust

#### Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Believing you must be healed to minister healing
  **Solution:** Remember that Christ is a healer regardless of your condition; preach from scripture
- **Problem:** Letting doubt enter because of physical symptoms
  **Solution:** Separate your condition from God's unchanging character and word
- **Problem:** Hiding your sickness to maintain an image
  **Solution:** Be honest so people can see you suffering with them, which gives them hope

### Topic 5: Two Promises to God / Faithful Stewardship

#### Steps

##### Step 1: Make Your Promise to God After Failure

When Pastor Philip was two and a half years into his walk with Christ, he experienced an epic failure that hurt a lot of people, including family and church members. Staring at a podium in the back room of a church, he thought his ministry was over. Two years later, when asked to preach chapel after two years away from the pulpit, he fasted for a week, studied the text, and made a promise to God.

##### Step 2: Promise Faithfulness in Proclamation

His first promise was that if God would anoint him again to preach, he would be faithful in his proclamation until God called him home. He kept that promise for almost 20 years and intends to keep it by God's grace until he dies.

##### Step 3: Promise Faithfulness Regardless of Platform

The second promise came when his church changed its name from Victory to 2019 in January 2023. With only 183 disciples, preaching in an empty high school auditorium where you could come an hour late and still get a seat, he promised God he would be faithful to preach to whomever God put in front of him, whether the room was full or empty.

##### Step 4: Refuse to Bend the Scriptures

Along with his second promise, he pledged that he would not bend the scriptures. He would not give an account to people; he would give an account to the one who gave him a second chance. He is content to be misunderstood, persecuted, lied on, called a demon, or called a false prophet.

##### Step 5: Live as a Steward, Not an Owner

His whole ministry and whole life is a second chance. He intends to make good with his second chance. All the favor, platform, doors, and relationships are from God. He is just a steward, not an owner. He will not protect a platform he did not build or appease people who are not the judge he will stand before.

#### Examples

##### Example 1: The Second Chance Ministry

Pastor Philip says, "My whole ministry and my whole life is a second chance. I intend to make good with my second chance." After an epic failure where he hurt a lot of people, he thought his time serving Jesus was over. But the Lord met him, restored him, and gave him another opportunity.

##### Example 2: Preaching to 183 People

With only 183 disciples and seats everywhere in an empty high school auditorium, Pastor Philip promised God he would be faithful to preach to whomever God put in front of him, whether the room was full or empty. He would not bend the scriptures and would not be handcuffed by the approval of people.

##### Example 3: Content to Suffer for Christ

He is content to be misunderstood, persecuted, receive death threats, be lied on, be called a demon, be called a false prophet. He is content to suffer all of that for the name of Christ because he knows he is doing everything he can to be faithful to the one who gave him a second chance.

#### Best Practices

- ✅ Make promises to God that honor Him and keep them
- ✅ Be faithful in your calling regardless of platform size or crowd
- ✅ Refuse to bend scripture to please people
- ✅ Remember that all favor and platform is from God
- ✅ Live as a steward, not an owner of what God has given you
- ✅ Give an account to God, not to people
- ✅ Be content to be misunderstood for the sake of the gospel
- ✅ Do not protect platforms you did not build

#### Keep In Mind

- God gives second chances
- Your whole ministry can be a second chance
- You are a steward, not an owner
- You will give an account to the one who gave you a second chance
- The platform you did not build is not yours to protect

#### Security & Safety Notes

- Breaking promises to God breaks trust and forfeits anointing
- Bending scripture to please people compromises the gospel
- Protecting a platform you did not build leads to idolatry
- Giving an account to people rather than to God leads to compromise

#### Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Allowing past failure to disqualify you
  **Solution:** Remember that God restores and gives second chances; make promises to Him and keep them
- **Problem:** Compromising scripture to grow a crowd
  **Solution:** Be faithful to preach to whomever God puts in front of you, whether the room is full or empty
- **Problem:** Seeking approval from people rather than from God
  **Solution:** Remember you will give an account to the one who gave you a second chance, not to the crowd

### Topic 6: Pride and Arrogance / Stewardship of Favor

#### Steps

##### Step 1: Identify Your Greatest Fear

Pastor Philip's greatest fear is not death threats, opposition from false religions, or people showing up to disrupt gatherings. His greatest fear is pride and arrogance creeping up into his heart. Identify what you are most afraid of losing or compromising.

##### Step 2: Take Inventory of God's Favor

When you take inventory of all the ways God has worked in your life — your team, your church, your travels, the doors that have opened, the relationships you have — recognize that these blessings are from God. For a person who is not grounded, these blessings can make you think it is all for you.

##### Step 3: Remember You Are a Steward, Not an Owner

Jesus has done all of this for Himself. You just get to steward it. You are the donkey that Jesus is riding on. The people are screaming "Hosanna!" and laying branches on the floor for the person on top of you, not for you.

##### Step 4: Eat the Fruit, Stay Away from the Glory

When God blesses, remember He is not doing it for you. He is doing it for Himself. This is the instruction Pastor Philip gives to his worship leader: when God blesses the songs and the platform, remember it is for Christ alone. Eat the fruit, stay away from the glory.

##### Step 5: Hang Yourself on the Cross Again

When pride or arrogance creeps up, go back to the prayer room and hang yourself on the cross again. Say, "God, crucify this in me again and take this out of me again." Remind yourself that all God has done, He has done for Himself, not for you.

#### Examples

##### Example 1: The Donkey Illustration

Pastor Philip taught his church through Matthew for three years. At the end of Matthew, Jesus rides into Jerusalem on a donkey. If the donkey could talk, it might say, "Look at all these people throwing branches for me. They are screaming 'Hosanna!' for me." But the branches and the shouts are for the person on top of the donkey. We are just the donkeys Jesus rides on.

##### Example 2: The Worship Leader's Instruction

Pastor Philip privately told his worship leader Trey, "Your influence is going to grow and the influence of the team is going to grow. Eat the fruit. Stay away from the glory. When God blesses 28:19 Worship and the songs we write, remember God is not doing that for you. He is doing that for Him."

##### Example 3: Pride as an Insult to the Cross

When pride and arrogance creep up in Pastor Philip's heart and make him think the platform and favor are for him, that is an insult to God and an insult to the cross of Christ. That is when he is most afraid. God can turn off the favor to show you that you cannot do any of this in your own strength.

#### Best Practices

- ✅ Identify your greatest fear and make it spiritual, not physical
- ✅ Take inventory of God's favor and remember it is from Him
- ✅ Live as a steward, not an owner
- ✅ Eat the fruit, stay away from the glory
- ✅ Go back to the prayer room when pride creeps in
- ✅ Crucify pride and arrogance daily
- ✅ Remember God has done everything for Himself, not for you
- ✅ Do not protect platforms you did not build

#### Keep In Mind

- All favor and platform is from God, not for self
- You are the donkey Jesus is riding on
- God can strip away favor in a moment to show you your dependence on Him
- Pride and arrogance separate you from God and give Him no incentive to keep pouring out favor
- The greatest fear should not be death threats but pride creeping into your heart

#### Security & Safety Notes

- Pride and arrogance insult the cross of Christ
- Self-reliance and pride emerging from God's blessing separate you from God
- Forgetting that you are a steward leads to idolatry of platform and influence
- Trying to appease people rather than God leads to compromise

#### Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Forgetting that all favor is from God
  **Solution:** Take inventory daily and remind yourself that you are a steward, not an owner
- **Problem:** Taking credit for God's blessings
  **Solution:** Remember the donkey illustration; the glory belongs to Christ alone
- **Problem:** Being afraid of the wrong things
  **Solution:** Make your greatest fear pride and arrogance creeping into your heart
- **Problem:** Protecting your platform at all costs
  **Solution:** You did not build it; God can strip it away in a moment

### Topic 7: Judgment and Critique

#### Steps

##### Step 1: Establish That No One Is Above Critique

No one is above critique if they are living faithfully for Christ. Not a high school student, not a single mom, not a business professional, not an athlete, not a preacher. If they hated Jesus, they will hate you too. If they called Jesus a demon, they will call you a demon too.

##### Step 2: Separate Critique into Three Categories

Develop humility and discernment to separate critique into three categories: righteous or valid critique, slander disguised as critique (born out of jealousy and covetousness), and satanic attack where the devil is working through unsaved mouths to diminish your credibility.

##### Step 3: Listen to Righteous Critique

There are valid critiques that, if you are humble, you should listen to. Internalize them, take them to prayer, and use them to become better. If a critique is right, say, "This person is right. I need to take this to Jesus in prayer and change in this area."

##### Step 4: Discern Slander Disguised as Critique

Some critique is born out of the fallen nature of other human beings. People are jealous of your platform, covetous of your platform, and want to be in your seat but are not. The only way they can feel good about themselves is to tear you down to prop themselves up. This is slander hiding behind the guise of critique.

##### Step 5: Discern Satanic Attack

Some critique is highly satanic, where persecutions and lies are being spoken about servants of God to diminish their credibility so people will stop listening. The devil is hijacking unsaved mouths to speak evil about you so that unsuspecting people will turn away from the voice God has raised up.

##### Step 6: Do Not Let Critique Shut Down Your Ministry

Even if people call you a demon or a false prophet, remain faithful. Jesus was called a demon and a false prophet. What chance do we have? We just have to be faithful in the midst of it all and pray for people to have more discernment.

#### Examples

##### Example 1: Three Types of Critique

Pastor Philip identifies three types: righteous/valid critique where the person is right and you should change; slander disguised as critique where jealousy and covetousness drive the words; and satanic attack where the devil works through unsaved mouths to discredit a servant of God.

##### Example 2: The Pharisees as Sons of Satan

When Jesus was persecuted by the Pharisees, He called them sons of Satan. They were jealous of His ministry because it was outshining theirs. Satan was working through them to antagonize Jesus with their mouths in an attempt to discredit His ministry so less people would listen. They shut the doors of the kingdom in people's faces.

##### Example 3: Social Media as a Platform for Demonic Voices

The proliferation of social media has given a platform to fools and demonic voices. People call real men and women of God false prophets and demons because of a mistake or disagreement. Unsuspecting people without discernment watch or read these social media posts and comments and turn away from the voice God raised up.

#### Best Practices

- ✅ Accept that no one is above critique if living faithfully for Christ
- ✅ Separate critique into righteous, slanderous, and satanic categories
- ✅ Listen to valid critique with humility and take it to prayer
- ✅ Recognize jealousy and covetousness behind slander disguised as critique
- ✅ Discern when the devil is working through unsaved mouths
- ✅ Remain faithful in the midst of all critique
- ✅ Pray for people to have more discernment
- ✅ Do not let critique shut down your ministry

#### Keep In Mind

- If they hated Jesus, they will hate you too
- If they called Jesus a devil, they will call you a devil too
- Critique is inevitable for anyone living faithfully for Christ
- To avoid critique is to do nothing and be unseen and unheard
- The devil uses social media to shut the doors of the kingdom in people's faces

#### Security & Safety Notes

- Listening to slanderous or satanic critique as if it were valid damages your faith and ministry
- Responding to every critique gives power to the enemy's voice
- Shutting down your ministry because of critique stops the gospel from reaching people
- Assuming all critique is valid leads to unnecessary self-condemnation

#### Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Treating all critique as valid
  **Solution:** Separate critique into righteous, slanderous, and satanic categories with humility and discernment
- **Problem:** Responding to slander as if it were correction
  **Solution:** Recognize jealousy and covetousness for what they are and do not engage
- **Problem:** Shutting down ministry because of criticism
  **Solution:** Remain faithful and pray for discernment in those who are listening
- **Problem:** Taking on the approval of people as your standard
  **Solution:** You will give an account to God, not to the people critiquing you

### Topic 8: Accountability and Grounding

#### Steps

##### Step 1: Identify Who Can Tell You No

Pastor Philip's list of people who can tell him no is: God, Lena (his wife), his children, the elders in his church, his staff, his mother, family members who have his best interest at heart, and brothers in the faith who are men of God with his best interests at heart.

##### Step 2: Accept Rebuke from the Right People

These people can say, "Don't go there." They can say, "Pray about that." They can say, "You shouldn't have said that from the pulpit." They can say, "You should apologize." When they speak, listen.

##### Step 3: Let Lena Keep You Grounded

Pastor Philip's wife Lena is the most unimpressed person with his platform, fame, or success. She is not impressed with him at all. She was not even following his own podcast until someone on staff told her she should. She is strong, pure, wise, godly, holy, and beautiful, and she keeps him grounded.

##### Step 4: Welcome Correction from Your Community

Allow your children, elders, staff, family, and brothers in faith to rebuke you. Pastor Philip's children rebuke him. He teaches his sons that real men take accountability, know how to apologize, and own their mistakes. He tells them, "We don't allow excuses in this house. Own your crap."

##### Step 5: Cultivate Relationships with Men of God

Have brothers in your life, lateral relationships, men of God who have your best interests at heart. They can tell you no, and you should listen. If you do not have these relationships, you are vulnerable to pride and isolation.

#### Examples

##### Example 1: Lena's Unimpressed Grounding

Lena is not impressed with Pastor Philip's platform, fame, or success. She keeps him grounded. When he came home from ministry, she was not waiting to hear about the accolades. She was just Lena. This is exactly what he needs to stay tethered to reality.

##### Example 2: Children Who Rebuke Their Father

Pastor Philip's children rebuke him. He teaches his sons Malachi and Josiah that real men take accountability, real men know how to apologize, and real men own their crap. He tells them, "If you didn't take out the trash, own that. If you got into a beef with one of your sisters and were wrong, own that."

##### Example 3: Brothers in the Faith

Pastor Philip has brothers in his life, lateral relationships with men of God who have his best interests at heart. They can tell him no, tell him to pray about something, tell him he should not have said something from the pulpit, or tell him he should apologize. He will listen.

#### Best Practices

- ✅ Identify who can tell you no before you need them to
- ✅ Accept rebuke from your wife, children, elders, staff, family, and brothers in faith
- ✅ Let someone unimpressed with your platform keep you grounded
- ✅ Welcome correction rather than resisting it
- ✅ Teach accountability to your children
- ✅ Cultivate lateral relationships with men of God
- ✅ Listen when people who love you speak truth to you
- ✅ Be willing to apologize and own your mistakes

#### Keep In Mind

- Lena is not impressed with platform, fame, or success
- Your children can and should rebuke you

### Topic 9: Biblical Masculinity

#### Steps

##### Step 1: Recognize the Spiritual Attack on Men

Understand that the attack on men and biblical masculinity is strategic. The enemy is working to rob men of biblical masculinity and remove them from the lives of children, homes, and families. This is spiritual warfare, not merely cultural drift.

##### Step 2: Create Safe Spaces for Men

Men need community where they can confess and be honest about what they are struggling with without feeling judged, condemned, or persecuted. They need spaces where they can be their authentic selves and find voices that speak life into them and call them to who God has called them to be.

##### Step 3: Comfort Men Who Are Hurting

There is a category of men who are suffering, silent sufferers, doing everything they can to be husbands and fathers, who are just struggling and do not know what to do. They need comfort, love, healing, and people who will speak to the person God has called them to be.

##### Step 4: Rebuke Men Who Refuse Accountability

There is also a category of men who need to be confronted, rebuked, and challenged. Enough is enough. They need to stop being boys, care about their wives, girlfriends, sons, and daughters, and understand how their decisions affect the lives of those around them.

##### Step 5: Teach Men to Own Their Mess

Teach men to stop pointing the finger and blaming everybody for what they are going through. There is a category of men who just have to own their mess. Real men take accountability. Real men know how to apologize. Real men own their crap.

##### Step 6: Call Men to Intangible Desires

Men in their nature desire tangible things: cars, money, fame, status. These things stroke egos and help insecurities. But men need to be told to desire the intangible things that will make them better men on the inside first. Then it becomes much easier to be a better man outside to the people around them.

#### Examples

##### Example 1: The Absentee Father Correlation

Pastor Philip taught middle school for about four years. During that time, about 75% of his students lived in homes without their fathers. As he studied the psychological damage of that, he saw a clear correlation between the absenteeism of fathers and men and human brokenness in adults. This is strategic.

##### Example 2: Teaching His Own Sons

Pastor Philip teaches his two sons, Malachi and Josiah: "Don't make excuses. We don't allow that in this house. Own your crap." If you did not take out the trash, own that. If you got into a beef with one of your sisters and were wrong, own that. If you dropped the ball on something your mother and I told you to do, own that. Repent, cry, let the Father love on you like a son or like the father you did not have.

##### Example 3: The Systematic Attack

Pastor Philip says, "I think much of this is a systematic spiritual attack against men and to ruin the biblical masculinity of men." Women are struggling to find strong men of God who can lead but also be tender. The enemy is working strategically to rob men of biblical masculinity and remove them from the lives of children and homes.

- ✅ Recognize the attack on biblical masculinity as spiritual warfare
- ✅ Create safe spaces for men to confess and be vulnerable
- ✅ Comfort men who are hurting and silent sufferers
- ✅ Rebuke men who refuse accountability and responsibility
- ✅ Teach men to stop making excuses and own their mess
- ✅ Teach young men that real men take accountability and apologize
- ✅ Call men to desire intangible things that make them better on the inside
- ✅ Be a man of character, integrity, holiness, and purity

#### Keep In Mind

- The attack on men is strategic and spiritual in nature
- There is a correlation between absentee fathers and human brokenness
- Men need safe spaces to confess without judgment
- Real men take accountability, apologize, and own their mistakes
- We need godly men in this generation more than ever

#### Security & Safety Notes

- The enemy's strategy to remove men from homes and families is damaging a generation
- Men who refuse accountability hurt their wives, children, and communities
- The absence of godly men leaves women without partners who can lead and be tender
- Boys making excuses and not owning their mess perpetuates brokenness

#### Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Treating the masculinity crisis as only cultural
  **Solution:** Recognize it as a systematic spiritual attack and respond with prayer and intentional discipleship
- **Problem:** Only condemning men without comforting them
  **Solution:** Some men need rebuke; others need comfort. Discern the difference.
- **Problem:** Not creating safe spaces for men to be vulnerable
  **Solution:** Men need community where they can confess without fear of judgment
- **Problem:** Teaching men to desire only worldly success
  **Solution:** Call men to desire the intangible things that make them better on the inside
